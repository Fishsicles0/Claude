Changelog

What's new.

Every release, newest first. Each new feature ships with an automated in-game test where one is possible.

v1.16.1

Latest22 Nov 2026

A cleaner menu sidebar.

  • Each category now has a line icon instead of a letter badge.
  • The open category gets a soft fill and a thin outline in your accent colour, and its icon and name light up too.
  • Mod counts sit in small pills tinted with your accent colour.

v1.16.0

22 Nov 2026

Fewer particles, less fire, more FPS.

  • New Effects settings in Performance, on by default: a 32-block particle distance, hidden ambient particles (campfire smoke, ash, spores, falling leaves, drips), a quarter of the block-break dust, no rain splashes and no fire layer on burning mobs.
  • Hide Rain & Snow (off by default) skips drawing weather altogether.
  • Lighter Video Settings now also shortens the weather radius, like the game's own Fast preset.
  • Particles that were created directly, like block-break dust, now count towards Performance's limits too.

v1.15.0

22 Nov 2026

Themes and accent colours, and a clearer website.

  • Themes: choose Modern, Midnight, OLED or Classic in Settings. Classic brings back vanilla-style grey with square corners.
  • Accent colours: Red, Blue, Purple, Pink, Orange, Gold, Green or Cyan, across the menu, the HUD and the HUD editor.
  • Themes switch instantly and are saved with each profile.
  • Website: a privacy policy; recordings of the menu and HUD editor; all three GUI styles side by side on the showcase; the mod list now loads without JavaScript; and real release dates in this changelog.
  • Evade icon records are now deleted after 7 days without use, checked every hour.
  • Removed Anti-AFK, Breadcrumbs, TargetHUD and PvP Info. They gave an edge some servers don't allow, and Evade sticks to quality of life.

v1.6 – v1.14

22 Nov 2026

Evade Clips, voice chat, a benchmarked FPS boost and Evade icons.

  • Evade Clips: a companion app for instant replay (15 s to 10 min) with sound, full recordings, a clip library and an editor, set up by the installer. Evade clips kills, totem pops, deaths, wins and advancements automatically and drops a named marker on your timeline for each. Share links that play inside Discord and a private phone gallery come from clips.evadeclient.com. Without the app, the Clips mod still saves instant replays as MP4s.
  • Voice chat through Simple Voice Chat, which the installer adds from Modrinth. It works on every server that runs it; press V or type .voice.
  • FPS mods: the installer adds Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ImmediatelyFast, EntityCulling, MoreCulling, Krypton, BadOptimizations, Sodium Extra, Fast IP Ping and FastQuit from Modrinth, each checked against Modrinth's checksum and benchmarked with Evade. In our busy test scene the full setup roughly doubles FPS. It also sets low-pause memory settings and removes only the files it added when you reinstall.
  • Performance: Low FPS When AFK, a Low-End PC Mode, and a lighter HUD that spends 15–33% less time drawing.
  • Frame Time Graph (HUD): a live bar per frame with average FPS, 1% low and worst frame, so stutters show up as red spikes.
  • Evade icons: the Evade logo next to Evade players in the tab list, free for everyone and verified through Mojang's sign-in check. Switch off Show That I Use Evade to hide yours.
  • Low fire and no pumpkin blur are built in as Pack Tweaks.

v1.0 – v1.5

21 Nov 2026

The first release of Evade: a server-safe, quality-of-life client for Minecraft 1.21.11.

  • A full HUD: cooldowns, armour, keystrokes, FPS/CPS, coordinates, a stopwatch, saturation, an item counter and more, each draggable and resizable in the HUD editor.
  • The menu: List, Cards and Compact styles, a grouped sidebar, an All / Enabled filter and live search.
  • Profiles: named presets that each remember their own mods, settings, keybinds and HUD layout, switched in one click.
  • Controls & camera: zoom, freelook, toggle sprint, FOV changer, a custom crosshair, block outline and a through-wall third-person camera.
  • Visuals: NoFog, NoRender, Fullbright, Clear Water, 1.7 animations, Item Physics, Hit Color, EnchantGlint, ShinyPots, Chunk Borders, Damage Tint, scoreboard and boss bar tweaks, and particle controls.
  • Quality of life: waypoints, better tooltips, auto-reconnect, better chat, notifiers, AutoGG, AutoTip, a TNT timer and Nick Hider.
  • Performance: Evade's own FPS boost culls far mobs and block entities, limits particles and idles while you're tabbed out.
  • A one-click installer for Windows, macOS and Linux, with a SHA-256 checksum on every file. Evade runs on Fabric, keeps to its own folder and leaves your normal Minecraft untouched.
