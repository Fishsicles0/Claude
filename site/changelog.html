Changelog

What's new.

Every release, newest first. Each new feature ships with an automated in-game test where one is possible.

v2.9.2

Latest28 Nov 2026

Cleaner cosmetic names and five new pets that fit the world.

  • Five new pets: Jungle Parrot, Wolf Pup, Cave Bat, Baby Turtle and Pocket Axolotl, built like the other pets (they look around, blink and never clip into you).
  • Cleaner names: Ginger Cat, Ember Fox, Soul Wisp, Baby Dragon, Honey Bee, Swamp Frog, Wandering Ghost, Cat Ears, Pet the Cat, Wave, Salute, Bow, Monocle & Mustache, Party Hat, Comet Beanie and Witch Hat.

v2.9.1

26 Nov 2026

Every cosmetic upgraded, a much sharper shop, the Wardrobe preview fixed, and launcher and Clips fixes.

  • All 130 cosmetics upgraded to a stricter quality test that checks every side of every item: capes get a quilted, stitched lining and bound edges; sharper hats, masks, back items and pets; emotes get props and sparkles.
  • Sharper shop: full-resolution 3D previews with real lighting and glowing parts that actually glow, every item framed properly on phones, denser particle effects, and crisp 512 px pictures on high-resolution screens.
  • Wardrobe: tall hats and pets are no longer cut off at the top of the preview.
  • Launcher 2.7.2: checks for a new Evade and a new launcher every 10 minutes and right before Play, so leaving it open never keeps you on an old version; "Close it now" closes the old Clips app wherever it runs from.
  • Evade Clips 1.2.1: no more falling back to slow capture after an unrelated error, quiet (no sounds or pop-ups) while Minecraft is closed, and editor toolbars that wrap instead of overlapping.

v2.9.0

26 Nov 2026

Schematics, technical tools, 54 new cosmetics, rebuilt wings and launcher add-ons.

  • Schematics (new): load .litematic, .schem and structure .nbt files, see them as a hologram you can move, rotate and mirror, view one layer at a time, check your build (missing, wrong, extra blocks), and get a material list against your inventory. Save an area you built, and paste in singleplayer. It never places or breaks anything for you, and servers can switch it off.
  • Technical tools (new): Tech Info HUD (light, slime chunk, moon phase and more), Tech Overlays (slime chunks, random-tick range, region borders, block grid, beacon and conduit range, structure boxes in singleplayer), Placement Lock (keeps your placements on a line, wall or layer) and Break Guard (stops you breaking spawners, budding amethyst and unripe crops by accident). Light Overlay gets a "Tall Mobs" option.
  • 54 new cosmetics: new capes (all free), pets, hats, masks, back items, sword skins, effects and 12 new emotes, including animated Glitch Koi, Void Eye, Code Rain, Warp Speed and Bass Drop capes, the Storm Ring aura and the Wandering Ghost pet. New emotes can do handstands, float and bring props and particles.
  • Better cosmetics: every wing rebuilt with a real arched shape and layered feathers or membranes (none clip any more), the three crowns made clearly different, sharper hats and masks, and brighter emote and aura pictures. Every item now passes a quality test.
  • Launcher 2.7.0, add-ons: a new Add-ons tab installs Distant Horizons, Flashback, Litematica, MiniHUD, SkyHanni and Axiom from Modrinth in one click, warns about version clashes and offers to switch off an Evade mod that does the same job.
  • Shop: emote previews now play in full, including handstands and floating.

v2.8.0

25 Nov 2026

Rebuilt pets, sharper cosmetics, free capes, faster hotkeys, Evade Clips 1.2.0 and a safer launcher.

  • Pets rebuilt: all 7 pets redrawn as proper little creatures that look around and blink. They sit on your shoulder or float beside your head and never clip into you.
  • Sharper cosmetics: every regular item redrawn as crisp pixel art (capes have 4× the detail), bigger shop pictures, and bolder aura circles.
  • Every cape is free for every Evade player, including the animated ones.
  • Text Hotkeys: 0.1 s cooldown by default (as low as 0.05 s) and a new Kick Guard that only holds back a press that would get you kicked for spam.
  • Evade Clips 1.2.0: one download source for the app, the clips site is launcher-first and Minecraft-only, sharing only to secure servers with a warning before you change server, clear "no sound" and "storage full" messages, and ffmpeg is only installed when its checksum checks out.
  • Launcher 2.6.0: asks before updating itself and never downgrades, refuses downloads without a checksum, no more hidden PowerShell, a "Faster graphics card" switch, and no false "problems will stop the game" warning on fresh installs.
  • Mods: every setting now has a description, a new Playtime HUD, World Map moved to J (M is voice chat's mute key) with a one-time notice when two mods share a key, Mouse Movement for Keystrokes, Nether/End fog strength and "only while moving" Motion Blur.
  • Privacy policy updated: cosmetics send nothing while the Cosmetics mod is off and no longer check every 5 minutes; shop rate limits store only a salted hash of your IP address. Discover removed.
  • Website: clearer download page with launcher screenshots, better contrast, fixed laptop-width menu, a /discord page, a press kit and a changelog feed.

25 Nov 2026

Much sharper animated Special cosmetics.

  • Special cosmetics redrawn: all 10 animated items now have 2–4 times the detail and smoother, seamless loops (16–24 frames). Hearthfire has real layered flames and rising sparks, Riptide rolling waves with foam, Galaxy Flow a turning spiral galaxy, Polar Lights rippling aurora curtains, and the wings, crown, halo, aura and sprite all got new artwork.
  • Crisp in the shop: textures are drawn pixel-sharp on phones and desktops, and the Special items' button now reads "Coming soon".

v2.7.0

25 Nov 2026

Evade Shaders, 76 cosmetics, a new start screen, one-click hotkeys and more FPS.

  • Evade Shaders (free): our own shader pack, with Potato, Low, Medium, High and Ultra levels. Soft shadows, a real sky with sunsets, clouds, reflective water, light rays and more. On laptops it starts on Low. Turn it on in the launcher's Shader packs.
  • More shader packs in the launcher: Complementary Unbound, BSL and Sildur's Vibrant join Photon and Complementary Reimagined, all downloaded from Modrinth.
  • Cosmetics: 76 items (57 new): capes, wings, hats, masks, back items, pets, auras, trails, kill and win effects, emotes and sword skins. Sharper textures, glowing parts, capes that move like cloth, wings that flap faster when you sprint and fold when you sneak, and pets that follow you. 10 Special items are animated (prices coming soon).
  • New start screen: drifting embers, an animated logo, friends online, your character in 3D with your cosmetics, and a Light mode picked automatically on laptops. It now always replaces the vanilla screen, including for players whose settings had it switched off.
  • Text Hotkeys: up to 20 one-click hotkeys with an editor, Ctrl/Shift/Alt combos, labels, presets and server-only hotkeys, and a shorter, adjustable cooldown (0.5 s by default).
  • Faster: less work for the HUD every frame, quicker mod lookups, and benchmarks no longer slowed by the idle FPS cap.

Cosmetics and the Wardrobe.

v2.5.0

24 Nov 2026

More FPS on laptops, a better chat, Open to Friends, and a client that recovers on its own.

  • Tuned for laptops: on integrated graphics Evade now starts on a laptop setup (Max FPS, Render Scale 65% with Evade's new upscaler, unlocked FPS). FPS Modes also tune Sodium, the HUD is cached at high frame rates, on battery the FPS is held at 60 (you choose), Smooth Pacing evens out frame times, and .bench scene runs a repeatable 60-second benchmark with a shareable result.
  • Chat: chat heads, chat tabs (Party, Guild, DMs, Friends and your own), smooth chat and emoji shortcodes with a picker.
  • New mods: Look Info (the block or mob you're looking at, your tool and the light level, never anyone's health), Light Overlay (spawnable spots, never through walls), Item Highlight with a new item picker, HUD Toggle, and Clear Glass, No Map Bobbing, fishing line colour and spyglass options in Pack Tweaks.
  • Open to Friends: share your singleplayer world with Evade friends from the pause menu (turn on the "Open to Friends" extra in the launcher). They always click Join themselves.
  • Friends: see what friends are doing (menus, singleplayer, a server or AFK), player cards with add, invite, voice volume and hide.
  • Media: a session recap in Evade Clips (one video from the day's best moments, also vertical), and a replay render queue with share links.
  • Cloud backup (opt-in): your settings, HUD, profiles and waypoints, encrypted on your PC with a passphrase only you know.
  • Safe Start: if Evade fails to start twice, it starts with its mods off and shows what probably caused it, with a button per mod to turn it back on. Many features now switch themselves off instead of crashing the game.
  • Server API v2: server prompts, transfers you confirm, balance and timer widgets and player-card actions, with server plugin 1.1.0.

v2.4.0

24 Nov 2026

A World Map, Item View, damage numbers, five new widgets, a screenshot gallery, addons, and a launcher you can drive from the keyboard.

  • World Map: press M for a map of the ground you've explored, saved per server and world, with waypoints you can add, edit and target. It only shows surface terrain you've actually seen: no caves, no other players. The Minimap gets height shading, water depth, real biome colours and your explored tiles.
  • Item View: move, turn and resize the item in each hand, change the swing animation speed (only how it looks: attack timing is untouched), and hide your shield.
  • 1.7 Animations: the old sword block pose, red armour when hurt, the old hurt tilt, sneak camera and heart flash.
  • Damage Numbers and a hit marker: only for hits the server confirms were yours. Nobody's health is ever shown.
  • New widgets: Server TPS (estimate), Connection Watch (warns when the server stops responding), Network, Ping Graph and Paper Doll.
  • Screenshot gallery in game (.shots): browse, rename, copy and delete with undo.
  • Look and feel: title screen backgrounds (or your own image), custom menu colours with shareable theme codes, and HUD widget stacks that move together.
  • Addons: other Fabric mods can add their own mods and widgets to Evade's menu and HUD editor. Each one is labelled with the mod that added it. See For mod developers.
  • Evade Launcher: works fully from the keyboard (F1 lists the shortcuts), larger text and reduce-motion options, a light theme and accent colours, instance folders, pins and sorting, instance export and import as a .zip, and a quiet check for mod updates at start.
  • Streamer Mode now also hides friend messages and friends' servers.
  • More launcher tools: import your instances from another launcher, drag and drop mods, packs and modpacks onto the window, pick or pin a mod's version and undo the last update, new-instance templates (PvP, Survival + shaders, Building, Low-end laptop), memory advice for laptops with integrated graphics, optional extras (faster start-up, more FPS, faster singleplayer chunks), "Start in safe mode", and desktop shortcuts that start an instance in one click.

v2.3.0

24 Nov 2026

The biggest update yet: friend messages, a new HUD editor, OptiFine-style resource packs, and a launcher that stays with you while you play.

  • Friends: message friends and chat with your party (.msg, .reply, .pc, or the new Chat tab), Join a friend's server or Bring your party to yours (you always confirm first), and friends' names in your colour in chat and the tab list. Messages are passed on, never stored.
  • HUD editor: undo and redo, select several widgets and line them up, nudge with the arrow keys, lock widgets, right-click to add one, ready-made layouts and HUD codes to share yours.
  • New widgets: Custom Text with live values like {fps}, {ping} and {biome}, much deeper Potion Effects, and titles, the action bar and subtitles you can move and resize.
  • Colour picker: a full picker with transparency, hex codes and your recent colours on every colour setting.
  • Menu and keys: Tab completes dot commands, a Keybinds page that shows clashes, hold or toggle keys with Ctrl/Shift/Alt combos, pinned mods, share one mod's settings as a code, a Waypoint Manager with icons, clickable coordinates in chat, a first-run setup with "Tune for my PC", and Streamer Mode.
  • Graphics: resource packs with connected textures, custom items, mob textures and models, and custom skies now work (the launcher adds the mods that read them), optional dynamic lights, a picker for any shader pack, Benchmark Every FPS Mode, Borderless Fullscreen, and the new 1.21.11 video options in every FPS Mode.
  • Chat and tab list: Coords Guard stops you posting your own coordinates by accident, a Hypixel chat clean-up, ping colours, friends or yourself first, and map, book and banner previews in tooltips.
  • Evade Launcher: stays open after Play and shows when you're playing, play time and a Stop button; a Game page with live logs (share them safely) and crash help with one-click fixes; per-instance memory, Java, window size and icon; resource packs, shaders, data packs and modpacks from Modrinth; Update all; mod checks; world backups; and modpack import and export.
  • Fixes: Play now always opens the Evade profile (an already-open Minecraft Launcher used to start an older one), the "old Evade Clips app is still running" warning no longer appears by mistake, and Evade Clips can record your game's sound on Windows.

v2.2.0

24 Nov 2026

Friends, parties and pings, more FPS on laptops, and a brand-new look from start-up to launcher.

  • Friends and parties: add friends by Minecraft name, see who's online and where, and invite them to a party. Open Friends in the Evade menu, or type .friend and .party.
  • Party Ping: in a party, click the middle mouse button to mark the spot you're looking at. Party members on the same server see a marker with your name, distance and coordinates, a pop-up and a sound. Hold Shift for a danger ping.
  • Render Scale (Performance): the world draws at a lower resolution and is sharpened, while the HUD, menus and text stay crisp. A big FPS boost in fullscreen on laptops. Balanced uses 80%, Max FPS 60%.
  • Start-up: an animated Evade loading screen, a new Evade main menu with your recent servers and live player counts, an Evade window icon and window title.
  • Evade Launcher: a clean new design, real icons for every mod, your skin on the home screen, a Discord button, and a one-click fix when the old separate Evade Clips is still running.
  • Evade Clips: recording on Windows works again (newer ffmpeg refused one setting, so nothing was saved), and the "Recording saved" pop-up no longer leaves garbage on screen.

v2.1.0

24 Nov 2026

The Evade Launcher, a mod browser, Hypixel tools and a server API.

  • Evade Launcher: the installer is now a full launcher with Evade Clips built in. One app: a home screen with your servers and news, instances (separate setups with their own mods and settings), a mod browser, and Clips' library, recorder and editor. Play opens the Minecraft Launcher with your instance selected, so sign-in stays with Microsoft. It updates itself.
  • Mod Browser in game and in the launcher: search Modrinth and install Fabric mods with one click, with their required libraries, each checked before it's installed.
  • Hypixel: a Quick Play menu, BedWars and SkyWars stats in the tab list (with your own Hypixel API key) and a BedWars helper that tracks generator timers and team upgrades.
  • New mods: Snaplook, Text Hotkeys, Totem Counter, Day Counter, Menu Blur, an Evade title screen, pack favourites and Discord status.
  • Evade Server API: servers can show waypoints, cooldowns, notifications, titles, team markers, borders, beams and holograms to Evade players, and switch mods off. A free server plugin sets it up.

v2.0.1

23 Nov 2026

Updating is painless, and one mod removed.

  • The installer updates itself: open the copy you already have and it fetches the newest installer from evadeclient.com, checks it and switches to it. No more downloading it again.
  • Evade Clips updates even while it's open: the installer closes it, waits out antivirus scans, or installs the new version beside the running one.
  • Downloads always come straight from the latest build, never from an old cached copy.
  • Removed Nametags: restyling other players' name tags isn't allowed on many servers.
  • Fullbright now tells you when shaders are on, because shader packs do their own lighting and ignore it.

v2.0.0

23 Nov 2026

A new look, a much bigger FPS boost, deeper mods, a minimap, and Evade icons that work.

  • New look: smooth rounded shapes, soft shadows, sharp Inter text at every GUI scale, and animations throughout the menu: opening, page changes, scrolling, hover and every switch and slider. The three menu styles and the HUD keep their layouts, just cleaner.
  • FPS Mode (Performance): one-click Max FPS, Balanced (default) or Quality. Unlock FPS turns VSync off and removes the frame cap, and Render Distance Cap keeps far chunks from eating your frame rate. Turning the Performance mod off puts all your own settings back.
  • Benchmark: type .bench to measure your average FPS and 1% low over 30 seconds and compare with your last run.
  • Installer: on Windows, Minecraft now runs on your fast graphics card (laptops often picked the slow one). The Shaders option now installs Photon by SixthSurge, with Complementary Reimagined beside it as a lighter choice, and the new Shaders setting switches between them in game.
  • Minimap (new HUD element): terrain only, never players, mobs or caves, so it's safe on servers.
  • Deeper mods: about 1,050 settings now, up from 329. New: Watermark, Tab Tweaks, Hitboxes (restyles F3+B), Motion Blur, Screenshot Tools, Sound Tweaks and Camera Distance (replaces Camera Clip).
  • Evade icons now work: sign-in never succeeded before, so no icons appeared. Your account is now checked with the chat key Mojang gives every Microsoft account. Your login still never leaves the game.
  • Server friendly: servers can switch off any Evade mod for their players; those mods show Disabled by this server. AutoTip only runs on Hypixel, AutoReconnect gives up after a few tries and never rejoins after a ban, and Camera Distance stops at 8 blocks. See Servers.
  • Evade Clips now works only with Evade Client. It records Minecraft only, with support for other games removed. On Windows it records the screen Minecraft is on, so fullscreen works.
  • Hundreds of smaller fixes across the client, the installer and Evade Clips.

v1.1 – v1.18

23 Nov 2026

Evade Clips, voice chat, themes, shareable profiles and a benchmarked FPS boost.

  • Evade Clips: a companion app for instant replay with sound, full recordings, a clip library and an editor, set up by the installer. It clips kills, totem pops, wins and advancements automatically, and makes share links that play inside Discord.
  • Voice chat through Simple Voice Chat, added by the installer. Press V or type .voice.
  • FPS boost: the installer adds Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ImmediatelyFast, EntityCulling, MoreCulling, Krypton, BadOptimizations, Sodium Extra, Fast IP Ping and FastQuit from Modrinth, each checked against its checksum. Evade's own Performance mod adds Low-End PC Mode, low FPS when AFK or tabbed out, and Effects settings that cut particles, block dust, rain splashes and fire on mobs.
  • Themes: Modern, Midnight, OLED or Classic, with eight accent colours, a smooth Inter font, sidebar icons and a two-column Compact view.
  • HUD: per-element styling (Evade, Solid, Glass or Outline backgrounds, Chroma text, the smooth font), Hide In Menus and Only In Combat, a snap grid in the editor, Auto HUD Scale and HUD Size, the Frame Time Graph and the Frame Profiler.
  • Profiles: share any profile as a code, and link a profile to a server so it loads when you join. Search finds setting names, from anywhere with Ctrl+F.
  • Installer: optional shaders through Iris, a recommended memory size for your PC, downloads that can't stall, and clear help when Java is too old.
  • Evade icons in the tab list, Pack Tweaks (low fire, no pumpkin blur), a privacy policy, and fixes for Windows players, including the firework "Ticking Particle" crash and the menu on scaled screens.

v0.1 – v1.0

21 Nov 2026

The first release of Evade: a server-safe, quality-of-life client for Minecraft 1.21.11.

  • A full HUD: cooldowns, armour, keystrokes, FPS/CPS, coordinates, a stopwatch, saturation, an item counter and more, each draggable and resizable in the HUD editor.
  • The menu: List, Cards and Compact styles, a grouped sidebar, an All / Enabled filter and live search.
  • Profiles: named presets that each remember their own mods, settings, keybinds and HUD layout, switched in one click.
  • Controls & camera: zoom, freelook, toggle sprint, FOV changer, a custom crosshair, block outline and an adjustable third-person camera distance.
  • Visuals: NoFog, NoRender, Fullbright, Clear Water, 1.7 animations, Item Physics, Hit Color, EnchantGlint, ShinyPots, Chunk Borders, Damage Tint, scoreboard and boss bar tweaks, and particle controls.
  • Quality of life: waypoints, better tooltips, auto-reconnect, better chat, alerts for your own totem pops and low armour, AutoGG, AutoTip, a TNT timer and Nick Hider.
  • A one-click installer for Windows, macOS and Linux, with a SHA-256 checksum on every file. Evade runs on Fabric, keeps to its own folder and leaves your normal Minecraft untouched.
