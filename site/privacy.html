Privacy

Privacy policy.

Short version: no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling data. Some features need to talk to evadeclient.com or to other services, and this page lists every one of them. Last updated 25 November 2026.

Who we are

Evade Client is an independent project made and run by the Evade Team. "We" on this page means the Evade Team. Evade is free and needs no Evade account or sign-up. NOT AN OFFICIAL MINECRAFT PRODUCT. NOT APPROVED BY OR ASSOCIATED WITH MOJANG OR MICROSOFT.

For anything about your data, email privacy@evadeclient.com. That address is read by the Evade Team; you don't need Discord to reach us.

The client

Evade runs on your computer. Your settings, profiles, waypoints, maps, keybinds, replays and clips are saved in your .minecraft folder and are not uploaded unless you use Cloud backup or share a clip.

Evade sends us something only for the features in the table above. Each of them is explained below, with how to turn it off. We don't use any of it for ads, analytics or anything else, and we never sell or share it.

How Evade checks your account

Evade icons, cosmetics, Friends and Cloud backup need to know which Minecraft account you're playing on, so nobody can pretend to be you. Evade checks it without your password or login token:

  • Every Microsoft account gets a chat-signing key from Mojang; the game uses it to sign chat on servers. Its private half stays on your computer.
  • Evade asks us for a one-time code, signs it with that key on your computer, and sends us your player UUID, the key's public half, Mojang's signature of it, the key's expiry date and the signed code.
  • We check Mojang's signature with Mojang's published keys (we don't contact Mojang for this) and give your game a token that works for 24 hours. We don't store the token, the key or the signature, and we don't receive your username or e-mail address.
  • Offline ("cracked") accounts have no such key, so these features don't work for them and nothing is sent.

The check runs (about once a day, when the last token runs out) whenever one of these needs it: Show That I Use Evade while you're in a world, Friends once you turn it on, Cloud backup when you use it, and the Cosmetics mod while it is on. Cloudflare counts sign-in attempts per IP address for a minute to stop abuse.

Evade icons in the tab list

Evade players see an Evade logo next to other Evade players in the tab list. Settings are under Evade Icons.

  • Your icon (Show That I Use Evade, on by default): while you're in a world, the client tells us every few minutes that your UUID is using Evade. We store your UUID and the time we last heard from it, nothing else.
  • Other players' icons (Show Icons, on by default): every 30 seconds the client sends us the UUIDs in your tab list that it hasn't asked about in the last 5 minutes (up to 200 at a time), and we answer which of them use Evade. We don't store those lookups. Anyone can ask us whether a UUID is showing an icon, the same way.

How long: your icon shows for 15 minutes after the last check-in, and your record is deleted after 7 days without one. Switch off Show That I Use Evade while you're playing and it is deleted straight away.

Cosmetics in the game

The Cosmetics mod (on by default) shows Evade cosmetics on you and on other Evade players near you. Cosmetics are visual only and give no advantage.

  • Your cosmetics: while the mod is on, the client asks us, with the token from your account check, which cosmetics you own and wear: when you join a world or server, when you open the Wardrobe, and after you redeem a code or change what you wear. There is no regular check in between. When you change something in the Wardrobe, it tells us. Capes are free for every Evade player, so wearing one needs no purchase; players who wear nothing have nothing stored.
  • Players near you (Show Others' Cosmetics, on by default): to draw other players' cosmetics, the client sends us the UUIDs of players within your cosmetics Render Distance (32 blocks by default) who are in the tab list, up to 64 at a time and at most once a second. It asks again about each player about once a minute while they stay near and wear something, and about once every 5 minutes for players who wear nothing. We answer with what they wear and don't store the lookup. Players who don't use Evade just get an empty answer.
  • The catalog: when you join a world or server with the mod on, the client downloads the list of cosmetics, and the files of items it needs, from evadeclient.com. That sends nothing about you.

What you wear is public: any Evade player near you, or anyone who asks our API about your UUID, can see it. Switch off Show Others' Cosmetics to stop sending other players' UUIDs. With the Cosmetics mod off, the client sends nothing about cosmetics at all; turning it back on in a world asks once for the catalog and your cosmetics. What we store about cosmetics you own is under The shop.

Friends and parties

Friends, parties and party pings are off until you click Turn on Friends in the menu (or use a .friend, .party or .ping command). The client proves your account (see How Evade checks your account), then stays connected to us while you play. What we store, on our server at Cloudflare:

  • Your profile: your player UUID, the username your game reports (shown to other players, never used to identify you), your two privacy settings (whether anyone can send you friend requests, and whether friends see your server) and when we last heard from you.
  • Your friends list, friend requests, blocked players and party: as player UUIDs, with the time each was made. Party invites lapse after 10 minutes, and a party with nobody online for 12 hours ends.
  • The server you're on, only while you're online: the address of the multiplayer server you're playing on (not singleplayer, Realms or LAN addresses). We use it to deliver pings, and show it to your friends and party only if Share my server with friends is on (it is by default; switch it off in Friends → Settings). It's deleted as soon as you go offline.
  • What you're doing, only while you're online: whether you're in menus, in singleplayer, on a server or away (AFK), and since when. Your friends and party see it. Turn it off with Share what I'm doing in the Friends settings. It's cleared when you go offline.
  • Reports: if you report a player, we keep your UUID, theirs, what you wrote and when, for review, for up to 90 days.

Who sees what: your friends see your username, whether you're online, when you were last online and, if you share it, your server. Party members see the same. Someone who sends you a friend request sees your username and whether you accepted. Players can only find you by your exact username, and only while you allow friend requests. When you add someone by name, your game asks Mojang which account has that name, and it asks Mojang for the current names of your friends and of players who send you requests or invites, so names can't be faked; those requests go from your computer to Mojang, not through us.

Party pings are not stored. A ping (a position, a dimension and a kind such as "danger") is passed straight on only to members of your party who are online on the same server address as you, and to nobody else; your party members see your name, the position and its distance. Turning on a party with someone is both of you choosing to share these positions. Blocking a player stops their requests, invites, messages and pings.

Messages and party chat are not stored either. A message (plain text, at most 256 characters) goes from your game to us and is passed straight on to the friend you wrote to, or to the members of your party who are online, and to nobody else. It is only delivered to someone who is online at that moment: if they're offline it's refused, not kept for later. If their game is checking in every 15 seconds instead of staying connected, the message waits in our server's memory for their next check, 30 seconds at most, and is then gone. We never write the text of a message to disk or to logs, and we can't show it to anyone later; we only count how many messages you send in an hour, to stop spam. Conversations are kept in your game's memory while it runs and are never saved to your computer. You can only message friends and your party, and blocking a player stops their messages.

Open to Friends invites are not stored either. When you open your singleplayer world to friends, the tunnel address it gets is passed straight on only to the friends or party members you ticked who are online and haven't blocked you, and to nobody else. Joining always needs their click.

Join and "Bring party here": a Join button appears for a friend only when they share their server, and a party leader can send the party the address of the server they're on. Nothing joins for you: you click Join, check the address, and confirm. The address a leader sends is passed on like a message and not stored.

Friend colours (your friends' names in their own colour in chat and the tab list) and Highlighted Players work only on your computer; that list is never sent to us.

How long: everything above is deleted automatically once your account hasn't used Friends for 90 days. Delete my social data (Friends → Settings) deletes it all straight away; your friends simply see you removed. Switching Friends off disconnects you.

Why: to provide friends, parties, pings and messages, which you choose to use.

Cloud backup

Cloud backup is off until you press Back up. Your setup (every mod's settings, including a Hypixel API key if you entered one, the HUD layout, your profiles, the profile you picked for each server, and waypoints) is zipped and encrypted on your computer with a key made from a passphrase only you know, then stored with us under the player UUID your account check proves. We can't read it, and without your passphrase nobody can restore it. There is one backup per account, of at most 256 KB. It is deleted after 90 days without a backup or restore, straight away when you delete it, and with Delete my social data.

The shop and cosmetics you own

The shop isn't open yet. Cosmetics are optional and give no advantage in game. When you get cosmetics, we store, under the player UUID your account check proves:

  • What you own: the ids of the cosmetics on your account, how you got each one (a purchase, a code, or a partner server) and when.
  • What you're wearing: the cosmetic in each slot and its colour, if you picked one, free capes included. This is public (see Cosmetics).
  • Purchases (once the shop opens): the payment provider's order id, the player UUID it was for (and the buyer's UUID, for a gift), the items, the amount and currency, its status (paid, refunded or charged back) and when. Payments are handled by the payment provider under its own privacy policy; we never see your card details. If the provider sends us your name or email address, we don't store them.
  • Link codes: the 6-character code from Wardrobe → Link the shop that links the shop website to your account, for 10 minutes.
  • Redeem codes: which account used a code, and when.
  • IP addresses on the shop website: to stop people guessing link codes and to limit lookups, we count how often each caller tries a link code, a checkout or a cosmetics lookup. We never store the IP address itself: the count is kept under a salted one-way hash of it, for up to a day. The shop page keeps your cart in your own browser's storage.

How long: what you own and your purchase records stay as long as your cosmetics do (we need purchase records for refunds and our accounts); Delete my social data doesn't remove them. A refund or chargeback removes the items it paid for. Email us to have your cosmetics and records deleted, except what the law requires us to keep.

Why: to deliver the cosmetics you get and show them to other players.

Servers you play on

Like any Minecraft client, Evade talks to the servers you join. On top of that:

  • Evade hello: if a server registers the evade:hello channel (servers that support the Evade Server API), Evade sends it one short message when you join: that you use Evade, its version and which Server API features it understands. Servers that don't register the channel get nothing. Your Fabric mods also tell servers which plugin channels they understand, as all Fabric clients do.
  • Server prompts: if such a server asks you to type something or to move to another server, your answer (what you typed, or yes or no) goes back to that server on evade:reply. You can always cancel.
  • Server rules: a server can switch Evade mods off for its players. Evade only receives these rules; it doesn't report your settings back.

Discord Status

Discord Status (on by default) shows what you're playing on your Discord profile. It talks only to the Discord app on your own computer, never to us, and only if Discord is running. It sends: "Playing multiplayer", "Singleplayer" or "In menus" (or the server's address or list name, only if you switch off Hide Server Address), the Minecraft version, the elapsed time, the Evade logo and, unless you switch off Show Discord Button, a "Join the Discord" button. Discord then shows this to people who can see your Discord profile and loads the logo from its own servers, under Discord's privacy policy. Switch off Discord Status to stop it.

Replays and voice

  • 3D Replay (on by default): to let you start a replay mid-match, Evade keeps what the server sends you (the world, other players' names, skins and movement, chat and sounds) in a temporary file on your computer while you play, and deletes it when you leave unless you saved a replay. Saved replays stay in .minecraft/evade/replays and are never uploaded. They can contain other players' names and chat, so think before you share a replay file or a video of it.
  • Voice chat comes from Simple Voice Chat, a separate mod the launcher installs. When you talk, your voice goes to the voice chat of the server you're on, never to us. With Record Voice Chat on (the default), voices you hear, and yours unless you switch off Record My Voice, go into saved replays on your computer.

Other services

Some features talk to other services straight from your computer, under those services' own privacy policies. They see your IP address, as any connection does.

  • Modrinth, when you use the Mod Browser (and the launcher's Mods and content pages): your searches, the mods you install or update, and, to recognise the mods you already have and find updates for them, a fingerprint (SHA-1 hash) of each mod file in your mods folder. The fingerprint identifies the file, not you.
  • Mojang, only with Friends on: to look up the account behind a name you add, and the current names of your friends and of players who send you requests or invites, so names can't be faked.
  • Hypixel, only if you enter your own Hypixel API key: the key and the player UUIDs whose stats are shown. The key is kept in your Evade config and sent only to Hypixel (and, if you use Cloud backup, it is inside your encrypted backup).
  • Mojang and Microsoft's own services are used by Minecraft itself as usual (signing in, skins, chat signing); Evade doesn't change that.

The Evade Launcher and installer

The launcher downloads Evade, Evade Clips, the news on its Home page and update checks from this website; Fabric from fabricmc.net; Fabric API, Simple Voice Chat, the performance mods, the optional shaders and the content you pick from Modrinth; and on Windows ffmpeg (which Evade Clips records with) from gyan.dev. To show icons for the mods in a profile, it sends Modrinth the fingerprints (SHA-1 hashes) of those mod files. A modpack you import from a link is downloaded from that address.

Your skin in the launcher: the launcher reads your Minecraft name and player UUID from the official Minecraft Launcher's files on your computer (never your login tokens or e-mail address). To draw your face and skin, it fetches them by your player UUID from mc-heads.net, or from crafatar.com if that fails, at most once a day; those services see your UUID and IP address.

All these services see your IP address, as any download does, under their own privacy policies. The launcher sends nothing else about you and has no tracking.

Evade Clips

Evade Clips records Minecraft with Evade Client and edits on your computer. Nothing is uploaded unless you share a clip or turn on Send to phone (Settings → Sharing & phone), which shares new clips for you. Shared clips are stored at clips.evadeclient.com, which runs on Cloudflare. (If you set Evade Clips to use another share server, whoever runs that server looks after what you share there. Evade Clips asks you to confirm before switching, and only connects to servers that use https://.)

  • What sharing stores: the video and its thumbnail, plus its title, the game, its length, size and resolution, when you shared it, and whether it's a link clip or private. All of it stays until you stop sharing the clip, and is then deleted. An upload that never finishes is deleted after a day. Deleting a clip in Evade Clips removes its link too, unless you untick that option when you delete it. Each PC's account can store 25 GB of shared video for free (thumbnails don't count); when it's full, new shares are refused until you stop sharing older clips. Nothing is deleted to make room.
  • Your anonymous account: the first time you share, Evade Clips creates an account made of random codes, with no name or email. The server keeps those codes (the key only as a one-way hash), when the account was made and how much storage it uses.
  • Link or private: a link clip can be watched by anyone who has its link, and plays as a preview when you post it in Discord or other chats. A private clip only opens with its own private link: anyone you give that link to can watch it, but it shows no preview and search engines are asked not to list it. Switching a clip from link to private gives it a new private link.
  • Your phone gallery: every clip you share also shows up in a gallery on your phone, opened with the QR code in the app. Anyone with your gallery link can see all your shared clips, private ones included, and upload to it, so keep it to yourself. Reset phone link in Settings → Sharing & phone replaces it with a new one. Clips you upload from your phone are stored like clips you share.
  • Presets: when you share an edit preset, its name, description and the author name you type are public at clips.evadeclient.com/presets, with the date you shared it and how often it's been downloaded, until it's removed (ask us to remove one, or delete your shared data to remove them all). Which account shared it stays on the server and isn't shown.
  • Reports: anyone can report a clip or preset. We keep the reason, the details they type, when it was sent and which clip or preset it's about, but not the sender's name or IP address (see below). Reports are kept until a moderator has reviewed them, and are deleted along with the clip or preset.
  • Removed clips: if a moderator removes a clip for breaking the rules, its video, thumbnail and details are deleted. Only a note of which rule was broken, and when, stays on the server, so the link can say why. You get a notice in the app naming the clip and the reason; it stays with your account until you delete your shared data.
  • IP addresses: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver clips.evadeclient.com, block attacks and limit how quickly new accounts, reports and presets can be sent. The share server doesn't log or store IP addresses. To count each report and preset download once per person, it keeps a salted one-way hash of the address instead: with the report until the report is deleted, and for a day or two for downloads.
  • Downloads the app makes: on Windows, Evade Clips downloads ffmpeg from gyan.dev if you don't have it. The first time you use voice commands or auto-captions, it downloads the speech engine from Maven Central and the speech model from alphacephei.com. Those services see your IP address, as any download does.
  • Voice commands and auto-captions run fully offline on your computer. No audio leaves it.
  • Game events from Evade Client go to Evade Clips on your own computer and are never sent to us. Evade Clips only accepts them from Evade Client.
  • Post to Discord sends the clip's title, game and link from your PC to the Discord channel you set up. The webhook address stays on your PC.

This website

evadeclient.com and clips.evadeclient.com have no cookies, analytics or ad trackers. They're hosted on Cloudflare, which processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver pages and block attacks. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when your browser fetches them. The shop's 3D preview loads its viewer from cdn.jsdelivr.net, which also sees your IP address. A shared clip's page remembers whether you turned autoplay off, and the shop page remembers your cart, in your own browser's storage; that never leaves your device.

Your choices

  • Evade icons: switch off Show That I Use Evade while playing to delete your icon record now, and Show Icons to stop sending us the players in your tab list.
  • Cosmetics: switch off Show Others' Cosmetics to stop sending us the players near you, or the Cosmetics mod to stop every cosmetics request, your own included.
  • Discord Status: switch it off to stop telling the Discord app what you play; Hide Server Address is on by default.
  • Friends: Delete my social data in the menu's Friends → Settings deletes your friends, requests, blocks, party and cloud backup from our server at once. Switch off Share my server with friends to keep your server to yourself, Share what I'm doing to keep your activity to yourself, or Allow friend requests to stop new requests.
  • Evade Clips: stop sharing any clip at any time, or delete it together with its link. Shared clips that are no longer in your library can be deleted from the list of clips on the server in Settings → Sharing & phone.
  • Delete my shared data in Evade Clips (Settings → Sharing & phone) deletes everything sharing has stored for you, all at once: every clip, preset and notice, the reports about your clips, and the anonymous account itself. If you can't use the app any more, email us your phone gallery link (it shows the clips are yours) and we'll delete it all for you.
  • You can ask us what we hold about your UUID or your shared clips, or ask us to delete it, at the address below. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have further rights, including complaining to your data protection authority.
  • Evade is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data that identifies a child beyond the game's own player ID.

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and note it in the changelog.
