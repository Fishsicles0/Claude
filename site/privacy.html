Privacy

Privacy policy.

Short version: Evade collects as little as it can. No ads, no tracking, no selling data. Last updated 22 November 2026.

Who we are

Evade Client is made and run by the Evade Team. "We" on this page means the Evade Team. Evade is free and has no accounts, ads or analytics. It is not affiliated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft.

The client

Evade runs on your computer. Your settings, profiles, waypoints and keybinds are saved in your .minecraft folder and never uploaded. Clips the Clips mod records are saved on your computer too.

The only thing the client sends to us is for Evade icons, below. Switch off Evade Icons and the client sends nothing to us at all.

Evade icons in the tab list

To show the Evade logo next to people using Evade, without anyone being able to fake it, the client proves which Minecraft account you're playing on:

  • Your game asks Mojang's session server to "join" a one-time code from us, the same check every online-mode server uses. Your password and Minecraft login token go only to Mojang and never reach us.
  • We ask Mojang which account joined that code. Mojang tells us your username and player UUID.
  • While you play, the client tells us every few minutes that your UUID is using Evade. We store your UUID and the time we last heard from it. We don't store your username.
  • To draw icons, the client sends us the UUIDs of players in your tab list and we say which of them use Evade. We don't store those lookups.

How long: your UUID shows an icon for 15 minutes after the last check-in, and its record is deleted automatically after 7 days without use. Switch off Show That I Use Evade while you're playing and your record is deleted straight away.

Why: to provide the icon feature you can turn off at any time. We never sell or share this data, and we don't use it for anything else.

The installer

The installer downloads Evade from this website, Fabric from fabricmc.net, and Fabric API, Simple Voice Chat and the performance mods from Modrinth. Those services see your IP address when you download, as any download does, under their own privacy policies. The installer sends no personal data and has no tracking.

Evade Clips

Evade Clips records and edits on your computer. Nothing is uploaded unless you share a clip.

  • Sharing: when you share a clip, the video, its thumbnail and title are stored at clips.evadeclient.com until you delete it (Stop sharing in the app). Sharing creates an anonymous account made of random codes, with no name or email.
  • Link or private: "link" clips can be watched by anyone with the link; private clips only through your own gallery link.
  • Reports: if someone reports a clip, we keep the reason and the details they type, without their identity, so we can review it. A clip removed for breaking the rules is deleted, and you're told which clip and why.
  • Voice commands, when you turn them on, run fully offline on your computer.

This website

evadeclient.com has no cookies, analytics or ad trackers. It's hosted on Cloudflare, which processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver pages and block attacks. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when your browser fetches them.

Your choices

  • Switch off Show That I Use Evade while playing to delete your icon record now, or Evade Icons to stop all contact with us.
  • Delete shared clips from Evade Clips at any time.
  • You can ask us what we hold about your UUID, or ask us to delete it, at the address below. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have further rights, including complaining to your data protection authority.
  • Evade is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data that identifies a child beyond the game's own player ID.

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and note it in the changelog.
