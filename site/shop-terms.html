Terms of Sale

The rules for buying cosmetics in the Evade Shop. Please read them before you buy.

The shop isn't open yet. These terms take effect when checkout opens. Nothing can be bought until then.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. Who sells to you

The Evade Shop is run by Evade Client ("Evade", "we", "us"), contact privacy@evadeclient.com. Payments are taken by our payment provider (see section 4), which may act as the reseller of the items and show its own terms at checkout. These terms apply to every purchase you make in the Evade Shop at evadeclient.com.

NOT AN OFFICIAL MINECRAFT PRODUCT. NOT APPROVED BY OR ASSOCIATED WITH MOJANG OR MICROSOFT. Minecraft is a trademark of Mojang Studios.

2. What you are buying

  • Digital items. Everything in the shop is a digital cosmetic for Evade Client. Nothing physical is shipped.
  • Cosmetic only. Items change how your player looks to other Evade Client users. They give no gameplay advantage of any kind and do not change how the game plays.
  • A licence, not ownership. A purchase gives you a personal, non-transferable right to use the item in Evade Client on the Minecraft account it was delivered to. Items have no cash value and can't be sold, traded or moved to another account, except where a gift is sent at checkout.
  • Who sees them. Cosmetics are shown to players who use Evade Client. Players on other clients see your normal skin.
  • Capes are free. Capes are never sold. Every cape is free for every Evade Client player.
  • No random items. There are no loot boxes or mystery items. You always get exactly the item you choose.
  • Servers' rules. Servers may choose not to show cosmetics. Evade can't control what other servers allow.

3. Linking your account

You choose which Minecraft account receives an item by entering the link code shown in Evade (Wardrobe → Link the shop), or, when you send a gift, the link code your friend gets the same way in their game. Please check the account before you pay. We never ask for your Microsoft or Minecraft password.

4. Prices and payment

Prices are shown in the shop before you pay, in US dollars. Any sales tax or VAT that applies where you live is worked out and shown at checkout before you pay. Payments are handled by our payment provider (Tebex), which is named on the checkout page; we never see or store your card details. Items are delivered to the linked account once payment is confirmed, normally within a few minutes.

5. Refunds

  • Within 14 days, not equipped: if you haven't equipped an item yet, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of buying it.
  • Always refunded: charges made by mistake, duplicate purchases, and items that never reached your account.
  • Not working: if an item doesn't work and we can't fix it within 14 days of you telling us, you get a refund.
  • How: email us with your order number. Refunds go back to the original payment method, and the item is removed from your account.

Items are delivered straight away. In the UK and the EU, by completing checkout you ask for the item to be delivered at once and accept that this ends the legal 14-day right to cancel digital content once delivery starts; our own 14-day refund for unequipped items above still applies. Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have under the consumer law of the country you live in.

6. Chargebacks

If you dispute a payment with your bank or card provider (a chargeback), the items bought with that payment are removed from your account while the dispute is open, and permanently if it is decided in your favour. If you think a charge is wrong, please contact us first so we can help.

7. Under 18

If you are under 18 (or under the age of majority where you live), you need permission from a parent or guardian before buying anything in the shop, and they should read these terms with you.

8. Changes to items and the shop

We may update how an item looks (for example to fix a visual bug) and may stop selling an item. Items you already own stay on your account. If Evade Client or the shop is ever shut down for good, we will give at least 30 days' notice on evadeclient.com, stop taking payments first, and refund anything bought in the 30 days before the announcement.

We may suspend items from an account that breaks these terms or uses them to impersonate or harass other players.

9. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the country Evade Client is run from. If you live elsewhere, you also keep the protections of your local consumer law, and you can bring a claim in your own country's courts.

10. Contact

Questions about an order, a refund or these terms: privacy@evadeclient.com. Please include the Minecraft username the item was for and your order number, if you have one. How we handle your data is explained in our privacy policy.
