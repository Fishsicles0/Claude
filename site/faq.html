General

Is it free?

Yes. Every mod, Evade Clips, Evade Shaders and the launcher are free, with no account or license key. The only thing that costs money is optional cosmetics in the shop, and every cape is free for everyone. Cosmetics only change how you look to other Evade players, never how the game plays.

Is it allowed on servers?

Yes, on the great majority. Evade is a quality-of-life client: a HUD, zoom, freelook, a custom crosshair, waypoints and visual tweaks. There are no combat or movement cheats, no ESP or x-ray, and nothing that automates gameplay or hides from anti-cheats. Most big servers allow these. Freelook is one exception: some servers, including Hypixel, forbid it, so Evade switches it off automatically there. A few very strict servers ban any modified client at all, so always check the server's own rules — but you won't be using anything that's against the spirit of fair play. Server owners can switch off any Evade mod for their players; see Servers.

Who makes Evade?

Evade is an independent project made and run by the Evade Team. It is NOT AN OFFICIAL MINECRAFT PRODUCT, and NOT APPROVED BY OR ASSOCIATED WITH MOJANG OR MICROSOFT. Email us at privacy@evadeclient.com about anything; you never need Discord to reach us. To chat, get help or report bugs, join the Evade Client Discord (the Minecraft client, not the Roblox game Evade). Everything that changes in each release is in the changelog.

Is there an Evade Discord, and what does Discord Status show?

Yes: the official server is linked from evadeclient.com/discord. Search results for "Evade Discord" often lead to the Roblox game Evade, which is unrelated. The Discord Status mod (Misc) shows what you're playing on your Discord profile: "Playing multiplayer", "Singleplayer" or "In menus", the Minecraft version and how long you've played, with a "Join the Discord" button. The server address stays hidden unless you switch off Hide Server Address; Show Discord Button removes the button, and switching the mod off removes the status.

What data does Evade collect?

Very little. Your settings, profiles, waypoints, maps, replays and clips stay on your computer. No ads, analytics or tracking. Here is everything the client talks to, and when:

  • Evade, to check your account, whenever one of the features below needs it: your player ID and a one-time signature made with your game's own chat-signing key. Your password, login token and username never reach us.
  • Evade, for tab-list icons (on by default): while you're in a world it checks in every few minutes; we keep your player ID and the time we last heard from it, for up to 7 days. To draw icons it sends the player IDs in your tab list, which we check and don't store. Switch off Show That I Use Evade to stop the check-ins, and Show Icons to stop the lookups.
  • Evade, for cosmetics (on by default): while Cosmetics is on, it asks which cosmetics you own and wear when you join a world or server, open the Wardrobe, or change what you wear; there is no regular check in between. To draw other players' cosmetics it sends the player IDs of players near you, which we don't store. Switch off Show Others' Cosmetics to stop that, or the Cosmetics mod to stop every cosmetics request.
  • Evade, for friends, parties and messages (off until you turn on Friends): your friends list, requests, blocks and party, and the server you're on only while you're online, kept until 90 days after you last use them. Messages and pings are passed on, never stored.
  • Evade, for cloud backup (only when you press Back up): your setup, encrypted on your PC with a passphrase only you know, so we can't read it. Deleted after 90 days without use, or straight away when you delete it.
  • Servers you join: a short "I'm Evade" hello, only to servers that ask for it with the Evade Server API.
  • The Discord app on your PC, for Discord Status (on by default): what you're playing, with the server address hidden unless you choose to show it.
  • Modrinth, when you use the Mod Browser to find, install or update mods (including fingerprints of the mod files you have, to recognise them).
  • Mojang, to look up the accounts behind friends' names.
  • Hypixel, only if you enter your own Hypixel API key, to show stats.
  • Evade Clips on your own computer, while it's open. Clips leave your PC only when you share one from Evade Clips.

The launcher also loads your skin picture from mc-heads.net (or crafatar.com) by your player ID. The full details, and how to turn each part off, are in the privacy policy.

How do friends and parties work?

Click your name at the bottom of the menu's sidebar and turn on Friends. Add friends by their Minecraft name (they need Evade too), see who's online and which server they're on, and invite up to 7 friends into a party. In a party, press the middle mouse button to ping where you look: party members on the same server see a marker, a toast and a minimap dot for 10 seconds. See the docs.

Who can see my server and my pings?

Only your friends and party members see which server you're on, and only while you're online with Share my server with friends on (switch it off in Friends → Settings). Pings go only to members of your party who are online on the same server address, never to anyone else, and they aren't stored. Blocking someone stops their requests, invites and pings. Your social data is deleted after 90 days without use, or right away with Delete my social data. The details are in the privacy policy.

Are party pings allowed on servers?

Pings only share positions you and your party choose to share, like telling your team coordinates in chat; they don't reveal anything about other players. A server can still switch Party Ping off for its players, like any Evade mod; see Servers.

Which versions does it support?

Minecraft Java 1.21.11 with Fabric. The installer sets up the right Fabric Loader and Fabric API for you. Other versions aren't supported.

What's new in v2.9.2?

Evade ships 113 quality-of-life mods — the full HUD, zoom, freelook, waypoints and a World Map, Profiles, Clips and 3D Replay, friends and party chat — an FPS boost from Evade's own Performance mod, Render Scale and the FPS mods the launcher adds, and a launcher with crash help, logs, Modrinth content, worlds and modpacks. Every release is listed in the changelog; the docs explain how to use it all.

Does it work with other Fabric mods?

It's a normal Fabric mod, so it should run alongside most others, though every combination hasn't been tested. Add mods from the Evade Launcher's Mods page (or drop the file onto the launcher), which also warns about missing libraries and mods that clash. If something conflicts, the launcher's crash help usually names the mod; otherwise switch mods off one at a time to find it.

The game won't start since I changed something

After two starts in a row that stop before the title screen, Evade starts with all its mods off and lists the ones that were on, so you can turn them back on one at a time and find the culprit. You can also ask for that straight away with Start in safe mode in the Evade Launcher. See Safe Start.

Does Evade have voice chat?

Yes, through Simple Voice Chat by henkelmax, which the Evade Installer downloads from Modrinth for you. It works on every server that runs Simple Voice Chat and in worlds you host. Press V for its menu. See the docs.

Does Evade Clips work with other games?

No. Evade Clips works with Evade Client: it records Minecraft while Evade Client is open, and only Evade Client can save clips to it. Open Minecraft with Evade Client to record.

How much can I share with Evade Clips?

25 GB of shared video per PC, free, with no watermark and no expiry. Only shared videos count (not thumbnails, and never the clips on your PC). When it's full, new shares wait until you stop sharing older clips in Evade Clips (Settings → Sharing & phone → Clips on the server…); nothing is deleted for you. There's no paid storage.

Why do some clips have no sound?

Without Evade Clips running, the Clips mod's built-in recorder saves the last 10–120 seconds of video only, with no sound, to .minecraft/evade/clips. Keep Evade Clips running (it starts with the game from the Evade Launcher) for clips with game, mic and voice-chat sound.

Is there a Bedrock or Xbox version?

No, and it isn't possible. Evade is a Java Edition Fabric mod. Bedrock (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, mobile, Windows 10/11) is a closed platform — you can't install a custom client or mod on a console at all. Anything that claims to add a "client" to a console actually routes your connection through a proxy that rewrites your packets, which is both a different kind of software and squarely in cheating territory. We won't build that.

Is Evade just quality of life?

Yes. Evade adds a HUD, zoom, cosmetic-style tweaks and quality-of-life mods on top of vanilla. It's a Fabric mod, so it runs alongside your other Fabric mods, and it's just quality of life: nothing that changes how the game plays or gets around a server's rules.

What's the icon next to some names in the tab list?

That player uses Evade: it's the Evade logo, and every Evade player gets it for free. Icons are verified with the chat-signing key Mojang gives every Microsoft account, so they can't be faked, and Evade never sees your password, login token or username. To hide yours, switch off Show That I Use Evade under Evade Icons. See the docs.

How does Evade boost FPS?

Two ways that stack. The Evade Installer adds the best-known performance mods — Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ImmediatelyFast, EntityCulling, MoreCulling (with its library, Cloth Config), Krypton, BadOptimizations and Sodium Extra, plus Fast IP Ping and FastQuit for a quicker server list and instant world quitting — downloaded straight from Modrinth and tested with Evade (switch off FPS mods to skip them). On top of that, Evade's own Performance mod skips far mobs and block entities, drops far and purely decorative particles (smoke, ash, falling leaves, rain splashes), cuts block-break dust, skips the fire on burning mobs and idles when you tab out. In our busy test scene the full setup takes Evade from about 21–25 FPS to 32–34, and it idles at low FPS when you're AFK or tabbed out. Pick an FPS Mode under Performance: Max FPS, Balanced (the default) or Quality. Unlock FPS turns VSync off and removes the frame cap, and on Windows the installer makes Minecraft use your fast graphics card. Type .bench to measure your FPS and compare settings. For shaders, switch on Shaders in the installer to add Iris with Photon by SixthSurge and the lighter Complementary Reimagined. Shaders are off by default because they cost a lot of FPS. Switch between them with the Shaders setting under Performance; see the docs.

How do I get the most FPS on a laptop?

Plug it in, and set Windows to Settings → System → Power → Power mode → Best performance; on battery, Windows can halve your frame rate. Keep your graphics driver up to date. With built-in graphics (like AMD Radeon or Intel Iris Xe), use FPS Mode Balanced or Max FPS and leave shaders off. The installer gives Minecraft 4 GB of memory on a 16 GB laptop, which is right: built-in graphics borrow the rest. Two memory sticks (dual channel) are much faster for built-in graphics than one.

Installing & updating

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

That appears for new apps that aren't code-signed yet: the Evade Launcher isn't signed, so Windows can't show a publisher. Only run a copy you downloaded from evadeclient.com, and check it first: in a terminal, certutil -hashfile Evade-Installer.exe SHA256 must print the SHA-256 listed on the download page. Then click More info, then Run anyway. What the launcher changes on your PC is listed under Is it safe to install?

The Mac or Linux installer won't open

The .jar launcher opens with Java 17 or newer, but Minecraft 1.21.11 itself needs Java 21, so install Java 21 from adoptium.net, then double-click the jar, or run java -jar EvadeInstaller.jar.

"I have Java 17 but it won't open"

Your computer probably has an older Java too (often Java 8), and that one is set to open .jar files. The Evade Launcher and Evade Clips now tell you when that happens. Install Java 21 (recommended: Java 17 opens the launcher, but Minecraft 1.21.11 needs 21) from adoptium.net (it becomes the default), or right-click the file and choose Open with your newer Java. On Windows, the .exe downloads avoid this: they find the Java the Minecraft Launcher installs.

How do I update?

Press Play in the Evade Launcher: when Evade, Fabric or one of the extras has a new version, it updates them first. When a newer Evade Launcher is out, it asks first; it checks the new file's SHA-256 before running it, and never installs an older version than the one you have (switch the question off under Settings → Launcher updates). Your settings are kept. Mods you added yourself update from the Mods page with Update all.

I don't see "Evade Client" in the launcher

Close and reopen the Minecraft Launcher after installing. If you picked a different Minecraft folder in the installer, make sure it's the one your launcher uses.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit the Evade Launcher (right-click its tray icon, Quit), then delete:

  • inside .minecraft: the evade folder, the evade-instances folder (your other instances, with their worlds: back up what you want to keep) and every versions/evade-client-* folder;
  • the Evade Client installations in the Minecraft Launcher (or restore launcher_profiles.json.evade-backup);
  • the launcher's own settings: %APPDATA%\EvadeLauncher on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/EvadeLauncher on macOS, ~/.config/evade-launcher on Linux;
  • Evade Clips' data (settings, ffmpeg, voice-command models; your clips too if they're stored there): %APPDATA%\EvadeClips and %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Evade Clips on Windows, ~/.evade-clips elsewhere;
  • any shortcuts you made (desktop instance shortcuts, and an Evade Clips entry in the Start menu or its Startup folder from the old separate Clips app);
  • on Windows, optionally, the graphics preference: switch off Settings → Faster graphics card in the launcher before deleting it, or remove the Java entries Evade added under Settings → System → Display → Graphics.

Then delete the launcher file itself.

Safety & settings

Is it safe to install?

Here is everything the Evade Launcher changes on your PC:

  • .minecraft: an evade folder (and evade-instances for extra instances), a versions/evade-client-* folder per instance, and one profile per instance in launcher_profiles.json, which it backs up first (launcher_profiles.json.evade-backup).
  • Its own data: settings in %APPDATA%\EvadeLauncher (Windows) or your user's config folder; Evade Clips' settings, clips index, ffmpeg and voice-command models in %APPDATA%\EvadeClips or ~/.evade-clips.
  • Windows graphics setting: it sets "High performance" for the Java the Minecraft Launcher installs (the same choice as Settings → System → Display → Graphics), so laptops use their fast graphics card. A choice you already made is kept, and switching off Faster graphics card in the launcher's Settings removes what Evade added.
  • The Minecraft Launcher: when you press Play and the Minecraft Launcher is already open with no game running, Evade closes and reopens it so it sees the new profile.
  • Shortcuts: none on its own. A desktop shortcut is only made when you choose Make a desktop shortcut in an instance's menu; Start menu or Startup entries left by the old separate Evade Clips app are pointed at the launcher.
  • Evade Clips: listens for its global hotkeys (only the keys set in its Settings: F7, F8 and F9 by default; nothing else you type is read) and, if you turn them on, voice commands that run offline.

Every download is checked before it's used: Evade's own files and ffmpeg against a SHA-256, Modrinth files against their SHA-512, and a file with no checksum listed isn't installed. The launcher asks before it updates itself and never installs an older version. The checksums are listed on the download page.

Where are my settings saved?

In .minecraft/evade/config/evade/config.json if you used the installer. Back up that file to keep your layout and keybinds.
