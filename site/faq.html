General

Is it free?

Yes. Evade is completely free, with no account or license key.

Can I get banned?

Yes. Most multiplayer servers ban cheat clients, and Evade does not hide itself from anti-cheats or staff. Every mod on the features page shows a ban risk: high (23 mods, such as KillAura, Fly, Speed, Velocity, Nuker and the Printer) are obvious to anti-cheats, medium (30, such as AimAssist, TriggerBot and AutoTotem) can be caught, and low (70, such as the HUD, Zoom and ESP) only change what you see. Low doesn't mean allowed: some servers ban any modified client. Use cheats in singleplayer, on your own server, or where the rules allow them.

Which versions does it support?

Minecraft Java 1.21.11 with Fabric. The installer sets up the right Fabric Loader and Fabric API for you. Other versions aren't supported yet.

What's new in v0.5.0?

35 new mods, bringing the total to 123: AutoWeapon, Offhand, SelfTrap, HoleFiller, NoSlow, InventoryMove, FakePlayer, AutoReplenish, Waypoints, HoleESP, LogoutSpots, BetterTooltips, AutoSmelter, AutoReconnect, BetterChat and more, plus a new Misc category. See the changelog for the full list and the docs for how to use them.

Does it work with Sodium, Iris and other mods?

It's a normal Fabric mod, so it should run alongside most others, but compatibility with Sodium, Iris and Lithium hasn't been tested yet. Put extra mods in .minecraft/evade/mods. If something conflicts, remove mods one at a time to find which one.

How does Evade compare to other clients?

Evade puts cheat modules and the PvP HUD of clients like Lunar or Badlion in one free Fabric mod. Some cheat clients have more modules (Meteor lists over 160) and some sell features meant to hide from anti-cheats or screen-shares. Evade doesn't include those and never claims to be undetected. Every mod here is listed with its settings and an honest ban risk.

Why isn't there an FPS boost or shaders?

Those need a different renderer. Install Sodium (FPS) or Iris (shaders) alongside Evade instead. Evade doesn't bundle other people's mods, including OptiFine.

Installing & updating

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

That appears for new apps that aren't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer only writes to your .minecraft folder.

The Mac or Linux installer won't open

The .jar installer needs Java 17 or newer. Install it from adoptium.net, then double-click the jar, or run java -jar EvadeInstaller.jar.

How do I update?

Run the installer again. It shows Update Evade Client when a new version is out and replaces the old files. Your settings are kept.

I don't see "Evade Client" in the launcher

Close and reopen the Minecraft Launcher after installing. If you picked a different Minecraft folder in the installer, make sure it's the one your launcher uses.

How do I uninstall it?

Delete the evade folder and versions/evade-client-1.21.11 inside .minecraft, then remove the Evade Client installation in the launcher.

Printer & schematics

What does the Printer do?

It places the blocks of a schematic for you, bottom to top, with the right rotation for logs, stairs, furnaces, slabs and so on, using blocks from your hotbar. It's in the World category. Like any auto-builder it's easy for servers to spot, so its ban risk is high.

Do I need Litematica?

No. If Litematica is installed and you've placed a schematic with it, the Printer builds that placement. Without Litematica, put .litematic files in your schematics folder and type .printer list to see them, .printer load <name> to load one, and .printer here to move it to your feet. A schematic loaded with .printer load takes priority over Litematica's.

Where is the schematics folder?

If you used the installer it's .minecraft/evade/schematics. Evade also reads .minecraft/schematics, the folder Litematica uses by default. Type .printer folder in game to open it.

How do I know which blocks I need?

Type .printer materials. It lists every block the schematic still needs and how many.

Texture Pack

Does the texture pack come with the client?

No. It's an optional, separate download on the download page. The client and installer never add it.

What does it change?

Menus, buttons, sliders, tooltips, the hotbar and crosshair turn black and red. It also lowers fire on your screen, makes diamond and netherite sword blades and ender pearls red, and puts the Evade logo on the title screen. It's made from Minecraft's own textures, recoloured.

How do I install it?

In Minecraft go to Options, Resource Packs, Open Pack Folder, put the zip in that folder, then move the pack to the right-hand list. It's made for Minecraft 1.21.11 and works with or without Evade.

Safety & settings

Is it safe to install?

The installer only touches your .minecraft folder: it adds an evade folder, one version file and one launcher profile, and backs up your launcher settings first. Every download is checked against a SHA-256 or SHA-512 checksum before it's used. The checksums are listed on the download page.

Where are my settings saved?

In .minecraft/evade/config/evade/config.json if you used the installer. Back up that file to keep your layout and keybinds.

Still stuck? Reinstalling with the latest installer fixes most problems.
