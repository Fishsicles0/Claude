Evade Clips · v1.2.1

Clip it. Edit it. Share it.

Built into the Evade Launcher: Evade Clips works with Minecraft running Evade Client. It keeps your last moments ready to save at a key press, a voice command, or automatically when something happens in game. Then you can edit and share them without leaving the app. Free, with no watermark and no ads.

Get the Evade Launcher macOS / Linux Open clips.evadeclient.com →

Evade Clips is the Clips section of the Evade Launcher: one download for the client and Clips. Close the launcher and it keeps instant replay running in the tray. Windows: nothing else to install (ffmpeg is fetched with Evade Client). macOS/Linux: Java 21 and ffmpeg (brew install ffmpeg or your package manager).
Already have the separate app? The launcher takes over its clips and settings. The older standalone app is legacy and will be removed in a later release; it's only for people who can't use the launcher: Evade-Clips.exe (4244 KB) and EvadeClips.jar (4132 KB), checksums on the download page. On macOS and Linux, use the launcher's .jar from the download page.

What it does

Never miss a play.

Everything runs on your PC, while you play Minecraft with Evade Client. It doesn't record other games. Nothing is uploaded unless you share it.

Instant replay

The last 15 s to 10 min of Minecraft, ready whenever Evade Client is open. Saving is instant because nothing is re-encoded. Encoding runs on your graphics card's video chip where supported (NVENC, AMF and Quick Sync on Windows; VideoToolbox on macOS; VA-API on Linux), so it works on NVIDIA, AMD, Intel and Apple without a vendor app.

Auto-clips

Kills, multi-kills, totem pops, wins and advancements (deaths too, if you turn them on), sent by the Clips mod in Evade Client. Events close together become one clip.

Hands-free

F8 saves a clip, F9 starts or stops a full recording and F7 drops a marker (change the keys in Settings). The hotkeys work while the game has focus because Clips listens for those keys system-wide; it reacts only to the keys you assign and never records or stores anything else you type. Or turn on voice commands in Settings (a one-time 70 MB download) and say “clip that” (or “clip it”, “save clip”, “save that”), “start recording”, “stop recording” or “mark that”. Speech is recognised offline on your PC, and only commands count, not chat.

Full sessions

Record whole sessions while the replay buffer keeps running. Every kill, pop and death is a named marker on the timeline.

Separate audio

Game, mic and voice chat each record to their own track, so you can turn your voice or your friends' voices down later.

Small files

Hard bitrate ceilings and HEVC: on the default Balanced setting, a busy minute of 1080p60 stays under 70 MB (about 40 MB with HEVC). Discord exports always fit in 10 MB.

Share links

Links that play inside Discord, with no watermark, ads or expiry. Private clips get a secret link of their own and never show a preview.

Your phone

Scan a QR code to see your shared clips on your phone, and upload videos from it straight into your library. Treat that code like a password.

Session recap

One button in the library turns the day's auto-clips (kills, pops and wins) into one captioned video, landscape or vertical for TikTok and Shorts, with a share link if you want one.

From 3D Replay

Renders from Evade's 3D Replay land in your Evade Clips library. Queue several (Replays → Queue render) to run one after another, each with its own share link if you like.

25 GB to share, free

Shared videos get 25 GB per PC (thumbnails and clips on your PC don't count). When it's full, new shares wait until you stop sharing older ones; nothing is deleted for you. There's no paid tier.

Server-safe

Evade Clips only records what's on your screen, so it shows nothing you didn't already see. Servers can switch off the Clips mod like any Evade mod; then auto-clips stop there and clips you've saved stay on your PC.

Without Evade Clips running, the Clips mod's built-in recorder still saves the last 10–120 seconds to an MP4 (480p–1080p), but with no sound. How it captures: Windows records the monitor Minecraft is on with Desktop Duplication (or the Minecraft window itself if that isn't available); Linux the window's area of the X11 screen; macOS the main screen. Phone or console clips: save the Xbox or PlayStation capture to your phone, then upload it from your phone gallery; videos already on your PC can be dragged into the library.

Editor

Edit like a pro. In minutes.

Everything is organized in tabs: Edit, Frame, Text, Effects, Audio, Export. Presets do a whole style in one click, at every kill.

Effects

Shake, punch, flash, hitmarker

Built-in sounds or your own, added at the playhead or anywhere on the timeline with a right-click. Speed ramps ease in and out of slow motion.

Vertical

Facecam on top, gameplay below

One-click 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts: crop, blurred background, or a split with your facecam over the action.

Speed ramps

Ease into slow motion on a kill and back out. Frame blending or optical flow makes new in-between frames, so slow-mo stays smooth.

Auto-captions

Speech to text on your PC: one word at a time that pops in, short phrases, or subtitles. Fix any word with a click.

Zoom & pan

Glide in on the kill feed, the crosshair or chat and back out. Keyframes ease smoothly from one to the next.

Blur areas

Hide names, chat or coordinates: draw a box and pick blur, pixelate or black.

Presets

Sniper ramp, Montage punch, TikTok facecam and more, applied at every marker. Save your own, send them as files, or browse the community's.

Sound library

Hitmarker, boom, whoosh, air horn, riser and more built in. Drop your own sounds in a folder and they show up.

After Effects & Premiere

Export a project folder: cuts and speed ramps as time-remap keyframes (ready for Twixtor), zooms, captions, markers and every audio track.

Exports that fit

Discord (always under 10 MB), TikTok and Shorts, YouTube 1080p60, GIF, or a lossless quick trim.
