Evade Clips · v1.0.0

Clip it. Edit it. Share it.

The companion app to Evade Client. It keeps your last moments ready to save at a key press, a voice command, or automatically when something happens in game. Then you can edit and share them without leaving the app. Free, with no watermark and no ads.

Library

Every clip, searchable

Hover to scrub through a clip. Filter by game, tag or favorite. Favorites are locked, so a bulk cleanup can't delete them.

Editor

Edit without another app

Trim, split, slow-mo, captions and meme text, facecam overlays, one-click vertical for TikTok and Shorts, montages.

What it does

Never miss a play.

Everything runs on your PC. Nothing is uploaded unless you share it.

Instant replay

The last 15 s to 10 min, always ready. Saving is instant because nothing is re-encoded, and it barely touches your FPS: encoding runs on your GPU (NVENC, AMF, Quick Sync).

Auto-clips

Kills, multi-kills, totem pops, wins and advancements in Minecraft. Kills, aces and full rounds in CS2. Kills to pentakills and steals in League. Loud moments in any other game.

Hands-free

Press F8, or just say “clip that”. Voice commands are recognised offline on your PC, and only the exact phrases count.

Full sessions

Record whole matches while the replay buffer keeps running. Every kill, death and ace is a named marker on the timeline.

Separate audio

Game, mic and voice chat each record to their own track, so you can turn your voice or your friends' voices down later.

Small files

Hard bitrate ceilings and HEVC: a busy minute of 1080p60 stays under 70 MB. Discord exports always fit in 10 MB.

Share links

Links that play inside Discord, with no watermark, ads or expiry. Keep a clip private, or let anyone with the link watch it.

Your phone

Scan a QR code to see your shared clips on your phone. Upload console captures from it, or plug in your PS5 or Xbox USB drive.
