How Evade compares with a typical Minecraft client.

PriceFree to play: every mod, Evade Clips and the launcher cost nothing. No mod or feature will ever sit behind a paywall; optional cosmetics may come later.
AccountNone needed. Typical clients often need one.
Instant replay & clipsBuilt in, with an editor and watermark-free share links, plus 3D Replay with camera paths. Typical clients don't include them.
Crash recoveryCrash help names the cause with a one-click fix, and Safe Start gets you back into the game. Typical clients rarely help here.
Verified downloadsSHA-256 checksum for every file. Typical clients rarely publish them.
