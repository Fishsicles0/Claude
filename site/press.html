Getting it right

Evade Client is a free Fabric client for Minecraft 1.21.11, Java Edition: 113 quality-of-life mods, no cheats, no account needed. The only paid things are optional cosmetics. It is not an official Minecraft product and not the Roblox game called Evade. Download link: evadeclient.com/download.
