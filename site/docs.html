How to use Evade.

Everything you need after installing: the menu, keybinds, chat commands and the tools that need a little explaining.

The menu Profiles Performance Voice chat Clips Default keys Chat commands HUD editor Waypoints Config files

The menu

Press Right Shift in game. The sidebar has 5 categories plus All Mods, Edit HUD Layout and Settings.

  • Left-click a card to turn the mod on or off.
  • Right-click a card, or click its gear, to open its settings.
  • Start typing anywhere to search all 65 mods.
  • On a settings page, click Keybind and press a key to bind it. Right-click, Esc, Delete or Backspace clears it.

Every setting and its default is listed on the features page: open any mod to see them.

Three menu styles. Under Settings → GUI Style you can switch between List (the default: spacious full-width rows with a description, keybind chip and settings), Cards (a grid of cards) and Compact (a dense single-column list). The All / Enabled filter at the top right shows either every module or only the ones you have on. The controls are the same in all three.

Profiles

Open Profiles in the menu to keep separate setups for different games. Each profile remembers its own enabled mods, every setting, your keybinds and your HUD layout.

  • Type a name and click Create to save your current setup as a new profile.
  • Click Use on any profile to switch to it — your current profile is saved first, so nothing is lost.
  • The × deletes a profile (you can't delete the active one or your last one).

Profiles live in config/evade/profiles/, one .json each.

Performance

Evade's Performance mod (Controls) is on by default. It skips drawing mobs and block entities past a distance you choose, draws one item per dropped stack, thins particles, idles at a low frame rate when you tab out or go AFK (back to full speed the moment you move) and can apply lighter video settings — all restored when you turn it off.

The installer also adds Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ImmediatelyFast, EntityCulling, MoreCulling (with its library, Cloth Config), Krypton, BadOptimizations and Sodium Extra, plus Fast IP Ping and FastQuit for a quicker server list and instant world quitting from Modrinth (leave FPS mods on). They stack with Evade's own mod; the FPS Mods Installed line in Performance's settings shows which are loaded. The installer also sets low-pause memory settings for smoother frame times.

Turn on the Frame Time Graph (HUD) to see how smooth your game really is: one bar per frame (green inside a 60 FPS budget, yellow slower, red for stutters), plus your average FPS, your 1% low (the FPS of your slowest frames, which is what you feel) and your worst frame.

Voice chat

Evade's voice chat is Simple Voice Chat by henkelmax. The Evade Installer downloads it straight from Modrinth when you leave Voice chat switched on.

  • It works on every server that runs Simple Voice Chat, and in singleplayer and LAN worlds you host.
  • Press V (or type .voice, or click Voice Chat in the menu) for its menu. The first time, its setup guide walks you through your mic and push-to-talk key.
  • On macOS, allow microphone access for Minecraft / Java the first time you talk.

Clips

Turn on Clips (Misc). While it's on, Evade keeps the last 10–120 seconds of your screen in memory. Press F8 or type .clip to save them as an MP4 in .minecraft/evade/clips; .clips opens that folder. Pick 480p, 720p or 1080p and 15–60 FPS in its settings. Video only, no audio.

.helpList every chat command

Every other mod starts unbound. Bind the ones you use most from their settings page or with .bind.

Chat commands

Commands start with a dot and are never sent to the server.

.helpList commands
.toggle <mod>Turn a mod on or off (short: .t)
.bind <mod> <key|none>Set or clear a keybind
.set <mod> <setting> <value>Change a setting, e.g. .set Zoom Level 4
.modulesList every mod, enabled ones in green
.voiceOpen the voice chat menu
.clip / .clipsSave an instant-replay clip / open the clips folder
.nick <name>Set the name Nick Hider shows instead of yours
.waypoint add <name> | del <name> | list | clearWaypoints (short: .wp)
.save / .loadSave or reload your config

HUD editor

Open the menu and click Edit HUD Layout. Drag elements to move them; they snap to edges and to each other. Scroll over an element to resize it. Each of the 24 HUD elements also has its own scale, background and colour settings.

Waypoints

Waypoints are on by default. .waypoint add Base saves where you stand; it shows as a red beam with its name and distance. They're saved per server (or singleplayer world) and per dimension. When you die, a Death waypoint is added automatically; turn that off in the Waypoints settings.

Config files

.minecraft/evade/config/evade/config.jsonMods, settings, keybinds, HUD layout
.minecraft/evade/config/evade/waypoints.jsonWaypoints

Paths are for an installer setup. If you dropped the jar into your own Fabric install, they're inside that instance's folder instead.
