Evade Launcher

Press Play. It does the rest.

Play updates Evade if needed, then opens the Minecraft Launcher with Evade selected. Sign-in stays with the Minecraft Launcher and your Microsoft account: the Evade Launcher never sees your password. Evade keeps its own folder, so your normal game isn't touched.

  • Instances, each with its own mods, settings and worlds, from templates for PvP, survival, building or laptops
  • Mods, packs and shaders from Modrinth, with Update all, pins and undo
  • World backups, live logs and crash help with one-click fixes
  • Evade Clips built in: instant replay, an editor and share links
What the launcher can add from Modrinth

Each is downloaded straight from Modrinth on your computer, and you can switch any of them off per instance.

  • FPS mods (on by default) for a big frame-rate boost: Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ImmediatelyFast, EntityCulling, MoreCulling (with its library, Cloth Config), Krypton, BadOptimizations and Sodium Extra.
  • Faster menus (part of FPS mods): Fast IP Ping for a quicker server list and FastQuit for instant world quitting.
  • Voice chat (on by default): Simple Voice Chat by henkelmax, so voice works on the many servers that use it.
  • Shaders (off by default): Iris with the shader packs you tick: Evade Shaders, our own pack, free with Evade Client (fast on laptops, with Potato to Ultra levels; the one switched on first, downloaded from evadeclient.com, or get the zip for your shaderpacks folder), Photon by SixthSurge and the lighter Complementary Reimagined by EminGT (all three ticked by default), Complementary Unbound by EminGT, BSL by CaptTatsu and Sildur’s Vibrant by Sildur. The other packs download from Modrinth when you press Play; Evade never hosts a copy.
  • Resource pack features (on by default), so packs made with connected textures, custom items, entity models and skies look as their artists made them: Continuity (connected and emissive textures), CIT Resewn Continuation (custom item textures), Entity Texture Features and Entity Model Features (random and emissive entity textures, custom entity models) and Skyboxify (custom skies, with its library YetAnotherConfigLib).
  • Dynamic lights (off by default): LambDynamicLights by LambdAurora, so a torch in your hand lights up the area around you.

Install guide

Up and running in two minutes.

Download the launcher

The .exe on Windows. On macOS and Linux use the .jar with Java 21 (Java 17 opens the launcher, but Minecraft 1.21.11 needs 21).

Click Install

The first time, it sets up Fabric, Fabric API, Evade and (if you leave them on) the FPS mods and voice chat in their own folder.

Press Play

The Minecraft Launcher opens with Evade Client selected. Press its Play button (sign in once if it asks).

Right Shift

Join a world and press Right Shift to open the menu.

Check a download before you run it: certutil -hashfile Evade-Installer.exe SHA256 on Windows, shasum -a 256 EvadeInstaller.jar on macOS, sha256sum EvadeInstaller.jar on Linux. The result must match the line above.

The Evade Launcher checks every file it downloads against its checksum before using it and won't install a file without one. When a newer launcher is out it asks before updating, checks the new file's SHA-256 and never installs an older version. The files keep their old names, so a copy you downloaded before becomes the launcher the next time you open it.
