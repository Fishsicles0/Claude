FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. Recording, editing and sharing are free, with no watermark, no ads and no account needed to record or edit. Shared clips get 25 GB of free storage.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 10/11, macOS or Linux PC. On Windows, Evade Clips offers to download the video engine it uses (ffmpeg) on first launch, and it needs Java 17 or newer, free from adoptium.net. On macOS and Linux, install Java and ffmpeg (brew install ffmpeg or your package manager), then open the .jar.

Will it slow my game down?

Barely. It encodes on your graphics card's dedicated video chip, saving a clip copies data that's already encoded, and instant replay only runs while a game is open.

Which games does it work with?

Any game you can see on your screen. Automatic clips for kills, aces, wins and rounds work in Counter-Strike 2, League of Legends and Minecraft, using each game's official interface where it has one. In every other game, Evade Clips clips the big moments it hears: fights, explosions, clutches.

Does it work with After Effects or Premiere?

Yes. Export → Export project folder gives you an After Effects script that builds the comp (each part time-remapped, so Twixtor and Timewarp work on it, plus zooms, captions and markers) and an XML that Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve import, with every audio track separate.

Where are my clips kept?

On your PC, as ordinary MP4 files in a folder you choose. Nothing is uploaded unless you share a clip or turn on phone sync.

Do share links expire?

No. A shared clip stays until you stop sharing it. You choose whether anyone with the link can watch it or only you can.

How do I report a clip?

Open the clip and tap Report this clip. A moderator reviews every report. If one of your clips is ever removed, the app tells you which clip and why.
