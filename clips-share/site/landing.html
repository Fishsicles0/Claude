Features

Never miss a play.

Evade Clips records Minecraft with Evade Client and runs on your PC. It doesn't record other games. Nothing leaves your PC unless you choose to share it.

Instant replay

The last 15 seconds to 10 minutes of Minecraft, ready while Evade Client is open. Saving is instant because nothing is re-encoded.

Light on your game

Encoding runs on your graphics card's video chip where it can (NVENC, AMF, Quick Sync, VideoToolbox or VA-API), so it works on NVIDIA, AMD, Intel and Apple, with no vendor app needed. Instant replay only runs while Minecraft is open.

Auto-clips

Kills, multi-kills, totem pops, wins and advancements (deaths too, if you turn them on), sent by the Clips mod in Evade Client. Events close together become one clip.

Hands-free

F8 saves a clip, F9 starts or stops a full recording and F7 drops a marker (change the keys in Settings). Or turn on voice commands in Settings (a one-time 70 MB download) and say “clip that” (or “clip it”, “save clip”, “save that”), “start recording”, “stop recording” or “mark that”. Speech is recognised offline on your PC, and only commands count, not chat.

Full sessions

Record whole sessions while instant replay keeps running. Every kill, pop and death is a named marker on the timeline.

Separate audio

Game, microphone and voice chat each record to their own track, so you can turn any of them down afterwards.

Small files

Hard bitrate limits keep a busy minute of 1080p60 under 70 MB on the default Balanced setting, and about 40 MB with HEVC. Discord exports always fit in 10 MB.

Your watermark, or none

Evade Clips never adds its own. Add your channel name or logo if you want, anywhere on the frame.

Session recap

One button in the library turns the day's auto-clips (kills, pops and wins) into one captioned video, landscape or vertical for TikTok and Shorts, with a share link if you want one.

From 3D Replay

Evade Client's 3D Replay renders straight into your Evade Clips library. Queue several renders (Replays → Queue render) to run one after another, each with its own share link if you like.

Server-safe

Evade Clips only records what's on your screen, so it shows nothing you didn't already see. Servers can switch off Evade's Clips mod like any Evade mod; then auto-clips stop there, and anything you'd already saved stays on your PC.

Without the app

If Evade Clips isn't running, the Clips mod's built-in recorder still saves the last 10–120 seconds to an MP4 (480p–1080p). Those clips have no sound; for sound, keep Evade Clips running.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. Recording, editing and sharing are free, with no watermark, no ads and no account needed to record or edit. Shared clips get 25 GB of free storage per PC.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 10/11, macOS or Linux PC with Minecraft Java and the free Evade Client, installed with the Evade Launcher. On Windows that's all: the launcher fetches the video engine (ffmpeg). On macOS and Linux, install Java 17+ and ffmpeg (brew install ffmpeg or your package manager), then open the launcher's .jar.

Will it slow my game down?

It's built to stay out of the way: it encodes on your graphics card's dedicated video chip where it can, saving a clip copies data that's already encoded, and instant replay only runs while Minecraft is open. We haven't published measured FPS numbers yet. If your game drops frames, lower the frame rate or quality on the Record page.

Which games does it work with?

Only Minecraft Java with the free Evade Client. Other games aren't supported. Evade Clips records the Minecraft window while Evade Client is open (Windows: Desktop Duplication, or the window itself if that isn't available; Linux: the window's area of the X11 screen; macOS: the main screen), and the Clips mod in Evade Client saves clips by itself on kills, totem pops, wins and advancements.

Can servers turn it off?

Yes. Servers can switch off any Evade mod, Clips included. Evade Clips only records what you already see on screen, so it never shows anything a server hides. When a server turns Clips off, auto-clips stop there; clips you've already saved stay on your PC.

Can I bring in clips from my phone or console?

Yes. Open your phone gallery (Settings → Sharing & phone → Show phone QR code) and upload a video from your phone: an Xbox or PlayStation capture you've saved to your phone, or a phone recording. It lands in Evade Clips on your PC. Videos already on your PC can be dragged into the library.

Does it work with After Effects or Premiere?

Yes. Export → Export project folder gives you an After Effects script that builds the comp (each part time-remapped, so Twixtor and Timewarp work on it, plus zooms, captions and markers) and an XML that Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve import, with every audio track separate.

Where are my clips kept?

On your PC, as ordinary MP4 files in a folder you choose. Nothing is uploaded unless you share a clip or turn on Send to phone in Settings.

How much can I share?

25 GB of shared video per PC, free. Only the videos count (not thumbnails), and clips you keep on your PC never count. When it's full, new shares are refused until you stop sharing older clips (Settings → Sharing & phone → Clips on the server…); nothing is deleted for you. There's no paid tier.

What does sharing store?

The video and its thumbnail, title, game, length, size and resolution, when you shared it, and whether it's a link clip or private, until you stop sharing it. Sharing needs no sign-up: Evade Clips makes an anonymous account of random codes, with no name or email. Shared clips that are no longer in your library can be removed in Settings → Sharing & phone, and Delete my shared data there deletes everything at once. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Do share links expire?

No. A shared clip stays until you stop sharing it. Deleting a clip in Evade Clips removes its link too, unless you untick that option when you delete it. A link clip plays for anyone with its link, right inside Discord; a private clip only opens with its own secret link and shows no preview.

What does the phone QR code give access to?

Everything you've shared, private clips included, and uploading videos into your library. Treat it like a password: don't show it on stream. If it gets out, press Reset phone link in Settings → Sharing & phone and the old link stops working.

How do I report a clip?

Open the clip and tap Report this clip. A moderator reviews every report. If one of your clips is ever removed, the app tells you which clip and why.
