Changelog

What's new.

Every release, newest first. Each new feature ships with an automated in-game test where one is possible.

v1.6.0

Latest{{DATE}}

Clips: instant replay, saved as MP4.

  • Turn on Clips and Evade keeps the last 10–120 seconds of your screen in memory. Press F8 (or type .clip) and those seconds are saved as an MP4 — HUD and all.
  • Choose 480p, 720p or 1080p at 15–60 FPS. Encoding runs in the background, so the game keeps playing while it saves.
  • Clips go to .minecraft/evade/clips; type .clips to open the folder. Video only for now — no audio.

v1.5.0

{{DATE}}

Performance: Evade's own built-in FPS boost.

  • Entity culling — mobs and items past a distance you choose aren't drawn (players can always stay visible).
  • Block entity culling — far chests, signs, banners and skulls are skipped.
  • One item per dropped stack, a particle budget, and a low frame rate while you're tabbed out to save power and heat.
  • Lighter video settings in one click (clouds off, no entity shadows, fewer particles), restored exactly when you turn it off.
  • In our busy test scene (900 armoured stands at 50–110 blocks) Performance took the game from about 17 to 29 FPS, the same across three back-to-back runs. Gains depend on your PC and how busy the scene is — in light scenes the difference is small.

v1.4.0

{{DATE}}

Fourteen new mods: a cleaner screen, better visuals and streamer tools.

Screen

Scoreboard, Boss Bar, Particles, Pack Tweaks, Clear Water, Inventory Blur

Visuals

1.7 Animations, Item Physics, Hit Color, EnchantGlint, ShinyPots

Info & tools

TNT Timer, Chunk Borders, Nick Hider

  • Scoreboard hides the red numbers, resizes or hides the sidebar. Boss Bar hides or resizes the boss bar.
  • Particles hides crit, sharpness, damage-heart, sweep and explosion particles.
  • Pack Tweaks lowers the fire on your screen and hides pumpkin blur. Clear Water removes underwater fog and the blue overlay.
  • 1.7 Animations brings back swinging while eating and the older held-item position. Item Physics lays dropped items flat.
  • Hit Color and EnchantGlint recolour the hit flash and the enchantment shine. ShinyPots makes potions glint.
  • TNT Timer counts down over primed TNT. Chunk Borders draws the chunk grid on one key.
  • Nick Hider masks your name everywhere on your screen (set it with .nick <name>) and can hide your skin — handy for streaming.

v1.3.0

{{DATE}}

Profiles: switch your whole setup in one click.

  • Profiles — save named presets (Default, PvP, SkyBlock, whatever you like) that each remember their own enabled mods, settings, keybinds and HUD layout.
  • Switch profiles from the new Profiles page in the menu; your current one is saved automatically before the next loads.
  • Create, name and delete profiles in place. Your active profile stays in sync with every change you make.

v1.2.0

{{DATE}}

Six new quality-of-life mods, with more on the way.

  • FOV Changer — lock your field of view so sprint and speed effects can't distort it.
  • Fullbright — maximum client-side brightness for caves and night (visual only).
  • Damage Tint — a coloured screen vignette that fades in as your health drops.
  • Protection — warns you when armour or your held item is about to break.
  • AutoTip — runs a tip command on a timer (e.g. /tip all).
  • Pack Display — shows your active resource pack on the HUD.

v1.1.0

{{DATE}}

A rebuilt menu — cleaner, faster and more spacious than ever.

  • New default List style — spacious full-width rows with a description, keybind chip, settings gear and toggle, so you can read every module at a glance.
  • Grouped sidebar with lettered category badges (Gameplay / Interface / General) and live module counts.
  • All / Enabled filter at the top right, and a wider search with fav: / recent: hints.
  • Three menu styles to choose from: List (default), Cards and Compact.
  • Larger, cleaner window with a softer glow and smoother open animation.

v1.0.0

{{DATE}}

The first release of Evade: a server-safe, quality-of-life client for Minecraft {{MC}}.

  • A full HUD ({{HUD}} elements): cooldowns, target info, armour, keystrokes, FPS/CPS, coordinates, a stopwatch, saturation, an item counter and more — each one draggable and resizable in the HUD editor.
  • Controls & camera: zoom, freelook, toggle sprint, a custom crosshair, block outline, no hurt-cam, and time / weather preview.
  • Visual tweaks: NoFog, NoRender, better tooltips, breadcrumbs, waypoints and a through-wall third-person camera.
  • Quality of life: auto-reconnect, better chat, totem-pop and other notifiers, AntiAFK and AutoGG.
  • One-click installer for Windows, macOS and Linux, SHA-256 on every file, and the optional Evade Texture Pack. Runs on Fabric, keeps to its own folder, and leaves your normal Minecraft untouched.
