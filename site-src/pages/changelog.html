v0.4.0

2026-11-20

50 new mods, the Printer, and the optional Evade Texture Pack. 88 mods in total.

  • New mods include KillAura, CrystalAura, AnchorAura, Velocity, Fly, Speed, Scaffold, NoFall, ESP, Freecam, Nametags, Nuker, AutoFarm and the Inventory Viewer HUD.
  • Printer: builds Litematica placements, or loads .litematic files itself. New schematics folder.
  • Movement, World and Player categories in the menu.
  • Evade Texture Pack 1.0, a separate optional download.
  • Installer 1.0.1 creates the schematics folder.
  • Website redesign.

v0.3.1

2026-11-20

New logo and wordmark, the Evade Installer, and evadeclient.com.

  • One-click installer for Windows (.exe) and macOS/Linux (.jar), with checksums for every download.
  • New Evade logo across the client and the website.

Earlier builds

The first PvP release on Minecraft 1.21.11: 38 mods.

  • Combat: AutoPot, AutoTotem, AutoMend, AimAssist, AutoCritical, AutoClicker, Reach, TriggerBot, Hitbox.
  • Visuals: ChestESP, ChunkLocator, PlayerTracers.
  • HUD: ArmorHUD for you and your target, cooldown timers for pearls, wind charges, spears and gapples, and 15 more elements.
  • The black and red menu, the HUD editor and quality-of-life mods such as Zoom, Freelook and ToggleSprint.
