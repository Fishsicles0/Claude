GUI styles

Three layouts. Pick yours.

Switch between them any time in Settings › GUI Style. Every mod, setting and keybind works the same in all three.

Themes

Four themes, eight accent colours.

Pick a theme and an accent colour in Settings. They restyle the menu and the HUD together, and switch instantly. Want the old-school look? Classic brings back square corners and vanilla-style grey.

Accent colours: Red Blue Purple Pink Orange Gold Green Cyan
