The facts

Getting it right

Evade Client is a free Fabric client for Minecraft {{MC}}, Java Edition: {{TOTAL}} quality-of-life mods, no cheats, no account needed. The only paid things are optional cosmetics. It is not an official Minecraft product and not the Roblox game called Evade. Download link: evadeclient.com/download.
