The Evade Client Discord.

Evade Client — the Minecraft client, not the Roblox game. This is the official Discord server for Evade Client. If you searched for "Evade Discord" and landed in a server about a Roblox game, that's a different, unrelated Evade.

Join the Evade Client Discord Read the FAQ first

How to join

  • Click Join the Evade Client Discord above. The only official invite is the one on this page, at evadeclient.com/discord. If an invite anywhere else says it's for Evade Client, open this page instead.
  • When you join, Discord first shows a short verification step. Finish it to see the rest of the server.
  • The server is new and small, so answers can take a while. For problems with the game, the FAQ and the docs are often quicker.

Stay safe

  • Nobody from Evade will ever ask for your password, your Microsoft login or a code from your e-mail, in Discord or anywhere else.
  • Download Evade Client only from evadeclient.com/download, never from a file someone sends you in a message.

Rather not use Discord?

You don't need Discord for anything. Email the Evade Team at privacy@evadeclient.com for privacy requests, problems with a server, or anything else. What the Discord Status mod shows on your own Discord profile is explained in the privacy policy.
