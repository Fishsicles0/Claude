    How it works

    Three steps, no passwords.

    The shop never asks for your Microsoft or Minecraft password. A short code from inside the game proves which account the items are for.

    Open the Wardrobe

    In Evade, open the Wardrobe and choose Link the shop. You'll see a 6-character code.

    Type the code here

    Enter it in Your Minecraft account. Codes expire after a short time, so get a new one if it stops working.

    Pick your items

    Preview anything in 3D, add it to your cart, or send it to a friend as a gift with their link code.

    Wear it in game

    Your items appear in the Wardrobe. Switch them on and off any time.

    Good to know

    Questions

    Do cosmetics give an advantage?

    No. Every item is visual only. Nothing changes your hitbox, reach, speed or anything else about how the game plays.

    Are capes really free?

    Yes. Every cape, animated ones included, is free for every Evade player and is never sold. Open the Wardrobe in Evade and wear any of them; you don't need to link the shop or buy anything.

    Who can see my cosmetics?

    Other players who use Evade Client. Players without Evade see your normal skin, so servers and other clients are unaffected. What you wear is public: anyone who asks evadeclient.com about your Minecraft UUID can see which cosmetics you have on (never your name, e-mail or anything else). See the privacy policy.

    Do I need to give you my password?

    Never. The shop only needs the 6-character code the game shows you in the Wardrobe. Nobody from Evade will ever ask for your password.

    I'm under 18. Can I buy?

    Only with permission from a parent or guardian, who should read the Terms of Sale with you first. They will be published here before checkout opens.
