# Evade cosmetics: files and format

Everything in this folder is generated by `scripts/build-cosmetics.py` (original Evade art, drawn procedurally) and is
byte-identical to the copy bundled in the client at `src/main/resources/assets/evade/cosmetics/`. Re-run the script
after changing an item; never hand-edit the generated files (their hashes are in `catalog.json`).

```
cosmetics/
  catalog.json          the catalogue: the backend serves it at /api/cosmetics/catalog, the shop page reads it
  <id>/item.json        what the item is (name, slot, rarity, colours, particle / emote data)
  <id>/model.json       cuboid model (not for trails, kill/win effects or emotes)
  <id>/texture.png      box-UV texture for model.json
  <id>/thumb.png        128 x 128 preview (Wardrobe grid, shop cards)
```

Served at `https://evadeclient.com/cosmetics/<id>/<file>`; that folder is the item's `assets.base`.

## catalog.json

```json
{"version": "starter-1", "items": [{
  "id": "ember_mantle", "name": "Ember Mantle", "slot": "CAPE", "rarity": "regular",
  "priceCents": 600, "currency": "USD", "partner": null,
  "colourable": false, "defaultColor": null, "description": "...",
  "assets": {"files": {"item.json": "<sha256>", "model.json": "<sha256>", "texture.png": "<sha256>", "thumb.png": "<sha256>"}}
}]}
```

* `id`: `[a-z0-9][a-z0-9_-]{0,47}`. `slot`: one of `CAPE WINGS HAT MASK BACK AURA TRAIL PET EMOTE KILL_EFFECT WIN_EFFECT
  ITEM_SKIN` (there is no nametag slot, on purpose). The shop lists KILL_EFFECT under "Kill effects" and WIN_EFFECT
  under "Win effects".
* `rarity`, one of three tiers:
  * `regular`: not animated. A cape's normal sway, wing beats, a pet's bobbing and particle effects don't count as
    animated. Every starter item is regular.
  * `special`: animated items (animated textures and the like). Prices are announced later: a special item with
    `priceCents: null` shows "Price coming soon" on the shop and can't be added to the cart or checked out.
  * `partner`: a partner server's item (`partner` set); may be unpriced, in which case only the partner server grants it.

  The old names (common, rare, epic, legendary) and unknown values are read as `regular` (or `partner` when the item
  names a partner) by the backend, the shop and the client.
* `assets.files` lists every file with its sha256 (lowercase hex). The client refuses any file, bundled or downloaded,
  whose bytes don't match, and only downloads from `https://evadeclient.com/`. Downloads are cached in
  `config/evade/cosmetics-cache/<id>/` and re-checked on every load.
* `priceCents`: the price in US cents (nothing is sold yet), or `null` for not priced / not for sale. Only items
  priced above zero can be bought. `partner` is `null` or `{id, name}`.

## model.json (`evade-cosmetic-model/1`)

Blockbench-style cuboids, Minecraft entity units (1 unit = 1 skin pixel = 1/16 block).

```json
{"format": "evade-cosmetic-model/1", "space": "entity", "texture": [64, 32], "textureScale": 2,
 "bones": [{"name": "cape", "attach": "body", "pivot": [0, 0, 2], "rotation": [0, 0, 0],
            "anim": {"type": "cape"}, "emissive": false, "tint": false, "alpha": 1.0,
            "cubes": [{"origin": [-5, 0, 1], "size": [10, 16, 1], "uv": [0, 0], "inflate": 0, "mirror": false}]}],
 "display": {"thirdperson_righthand": {...}, "firstperson_righthand": {...}}}
```

* `space`: `entity` (y points **down**, the player's front is -z, the back +z, as in Minecraft's entity models) or `item`
  (y up, 0..16, like an item model; item skins only).
* `attach`: the player part the bone follows: `body` (origin at the neck), `head` (origin at the neck, head spans y
  -8..0), `right_arm`, `left_arm`, `root` (model origin; the feet are at y = 24) or `item`.
* Cube `origin` and `size` are in the attach part's space (not relative to the pivot); sizes are whole numbers.
  `inflate` grows a cube on every side; `mirror` mirrors its UVs (used for left-hand copies).
* `uv` is Minecraft's box UV: the cube's net starts at `uv` and is `2 * (w + d)` wide and `d + h` tall:
  top `(d, 0, w, d)`, bottom `(d + w, 0, w, d)`, then on the row below: west `(0, d, d, h)`, north/front `(d, d, w, h)`,
  east `(d + w, d, d, h)`, south/back `(2d + w, d, w, h)`.
* `texture` is the UV space; the PNG is `textureScale` times bigger (2 here: twice Minecraft's pixel density).
* `pivot` and `rotation` (degrees: pitch, yaw, roll, applied Z-Y-X like Minecraft) turn the bone.
* `emissive` bones glow (full brightness, translucent); `tint` bones are multiplied by the wearer's colour
  (`colourable` items; their textures are drawn in light greys); `alpha` makes a bone see-through.
* `anim` (optional): `cape` (swings like a vanilla cape), `wing` (`side` 1 right / -1 left, `base` sweep-back and `amp`
  beat in degrees, `speed` beats per second; folds when sneaking), `spin` (`speed` degrees per second about y),
  `bob` (`amp` units at `speed` Hz), `sway` (roll by `amp` degrees), `pet` (bob plus a slow look-around).
* `display` (item skins): Minecraft item-model display transforms for the right hand; the left hand mirrors them.

Limits the client enforces: 32 bones, 256 cubes, coordinates within ±64, textures up to 2048 px, files up to 2 MB.

## item.json

```json
{"id": "...", "name": "...", "slot": "AURA", "rarity": "REGULAR", "colourable": false, "defaultColor": null,
 "partner": null, "description": "...",
 "fx": {"type": "aura", "particle": "dust", "colors": ["#FFB347"], "rate": 3, "size": 0.7},
 "emote": {"length": 1.6, "loop": true, "parts": {"rightArm": [[0, -170, 0, 15], [0.4, -170, 0, -15]]}}}
```

* `fx` (auras, trails, kill and win effects): `type` aura | trail | burst | spiral; `particle` dust | snow | end_rod |
  spark | glow; `colors` (dust colours); `rate` per second (auras/trails), `count` (effects), `size`, `height`
  (spiral), `spark` (burst adds sparks). Particles are spawned by the viewer's own client only.
* `emote`: `parts` maps head, body, rightArm, leftArm, rightLeg, leftLeg to keyframes `[seconds, pitch, yaw, roll]`
  (degrees, replacing that part's angles; smoothstep between keys). `loop` repeats until the player moves.

## Previewing on the shop

A viewer that reads these files draws each bone's cubes with the box UVs above, attached to the matching part of a
player model (64 x 64 skin), turning bones about their pivots. Item-space models (item skins) are held in the right
hand the way Minecraft draws a held item: the arm's transform, then a turn of -90 degrees about x and 180 about y, an
offset of (1, 2, -10) pixels, the `thirdperson_righthand` display transform, and -8 pixels to centre the 16-unit
block. A cape bone is turned 180 degrees about y and then tipped back a few degrees about x (x first), so it hangs
behind the body. `thumb.png` is a ready-made picture for cards: models on a player wearing the shop's preview skin,
emotes as that player in the emote's key pose, the pet on its own.

## Backend API used by the client

All under `https://evadeclient.com/api/cosmetics/`:

| Call | Auth | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET catalog` | none | the catalogue above plus `assets.base` per item |
| `GET players?uuids=a,b` (≤ 64, undashed lowercase hex) | none | `{players: {<uuid>: {equipped: {SLOT: {id, color?}}}}}`; players without cosmetics are left out |
| `GET me` | Bearer (Evade social token) | `{owned: [ids], equipped: {...}}` |
| `POST equip {slot, id|null, color?: "#RRGGBB"}` | Bearer | `{ok, equipped}` |
| `POST redeem {code}` | Bearer | 200 on success |
| `GET link-code` | Bearer | `{code, expiresAt}` (ms) |

The client asks about nearby players at most once a minute each (batches of 64, only players rendered near you and
in the tab list), and keeps the answer for 60 s.
