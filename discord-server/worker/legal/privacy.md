# Privacy Policy

**Effective:** November 21, 2026 (updated to cover warnings and reports)

This policy explains what the **Evade Client** Discord bot (the "Bot") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The Bot works only inside the Evade Client Discord server.

## What we collect
| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your Discord user ID, username and server roles | When you press a button, submit a form or run a command | To know who you are and what you're allowed to do |
| Your account's creation date (part of your user ID) | When you verify | To stop brand-new accounts used in raids |
| What you type in a ticket form and the messages in your ticket | When you open or use a ticket | So staff can help you |
| A transcript of your ticket | When it's closed | Kept for staff records, and sent to you by DM |
| Short-lived timers tied to your user ID | After a failed verification or a new ticket | To enforce cooldowns. They delete themselves within minutes |
| Records of moderation actions | When staff use a command, or when you verify | Posted in staff-only log channels for accountability |
| Warnings staff give you (reason, who gave it, when) | When a moderator warns you | So staff can see repeat problems. Kept until an Admin clears them |
| A message or user you report | When you use Report message / Report user | Copied into a private report ticket for staff |

We **don't** collect your email, IP address, payment details or messages outside tickets. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other sites.

## Where it's stored
- **Discord:** tickets, transcripts and logs are messages in the Server's private staff channels. Discord's own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy) applies to them.
- **Cloudflare:** the Bot runs on Cloudflare Pages. Cooldown timers, the ticket counter and server settings (such as raid mode) live in Cloudflare's storage. Cloudflare may keep standard request logs; see [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## How long we keep it
- Cooldown timers: a few minutes at most.
- Warnings: until an Admin clears them.
- Ticket transcripts and moderation logs: until staff delete them.
- Everything else lives in Discord for as long as the Server or the messages exist.

## Who can see it
Only Server staff who need it. Admins see everything. Mods see tickets and moderation logs. Developers see bug-report tickets. We only share data if the law requires it, or to report abuse to Discord.

## Your choices
- **See or delete your data:** open a ticket and ask. We'll delete your transcripts and log entries unless we need them to deal with abuse.
- **Stop all collection:** stop using the Bot, or leave the Server.

## Children
You must meet Discord's minimum age to use Discord, and so the Bot.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

## Contact
Open a ticket in the Evade Client Discord server.
